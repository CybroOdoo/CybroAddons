Frequently Asked Questions

Which documents can I reset in bulk?
Customer invoices, vendor bills and their credit notes/refunds. Misc journal entries are ignored.
What happens to records that can't be reset?
They are skipped and listed with the reason (locked period, hashed entry, not posted, needs a cancellation request…). One bad record never blocks the batch.
Who can use it?
Only members of the Accounting Manager group see and run the bulk actions.
Does it reset paid / reconciled invoices?
No — reconciled or paid invoices are skipped. Unreconcile them first, then reset.
