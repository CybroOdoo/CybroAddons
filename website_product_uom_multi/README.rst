Website Product Multi UOM
=========================

Select the Product's UOM before adding to Cart

Key Features
------------

* **Flexible UOM Selection on Product Pages**: Customers can choose their preferred Unit of Measure (Units, Dozen, Packs, etc.) directly from a dropdown selector on the eCommerce product page before adding items to the cart.
* **Instant Dynamic Price Recomputation**: Product prices update automatically and seamlessly on the storefront as soon as a different UOM is selected, applying standard unit ratio conversion in real time.
* **Separate Cart Line Splitting by UOM**: Adding the same product in multiple Units of Measure separates each selection into distinct cart and checkout lines with accurate per-UOM quantities and subtotals.
* **Seamless Checkout & Backend Integration**: Selected Units of Measure are preserved throughout the cart, checkout process, and resulting backend Sale Orders and Delivery Pickings.
* **Multi-Pricelist & Multi-Currency Compatibility**: Accurately scales prices across custom pricelists, volume discounts, and currency configurations without manual adjustment.
* **Zero Configuration Required**: Works out of the box with standard Odoo 20 Units of Measure categories and products.
