The Core Pillars

Why Oil & Gas Custody Transfer?

Ownership Separation

Track legal owner, custodian, operator and carrier as separate fields so responsibility is always unambiguous throughout the handover chain.

Configurable Approvals

Choose single or dual approval per transfer. High-value commercial transfers automatically require a second independent sign-off before they can proceed.

Delivery Validation Wizard

A built-in wizard captures arrival time, actual delivered quantity and on-site measurement values before stock is validated, keeping custody and inventory in step.

Full Audit Trail

Every state change, approval, dispute and resolution is stored with the responsible user, timestamp and old/new values for complete traceability.

Step 1 — Enable the Module

Enable Custody Transfer in Oil ERP Settings

Go to Configuration > Settings and tick the Custody Transfer checkbox under the Hydrocarbon Product Correction (HPC) section. Save to activate the module and its menu entries.

Step 2 — Access Custody Transfer Menu

Navigate to the Custody Transfer Menu

Open Pipeline & Transport from the top bar. The dropdown now shows a dedicated Custody Transfer section with All Transfers, Active Transfers, Completed Transfers and the Audit Trail list.

Step 3 — Flag the Stock Transfer

Flag the Stock Transfer as an Oil & Gas Transfer

Open a stock delivery order, check the Oil and Gas Transfer box on the form and add the product lines. This flag tells the system the transfer will need a custody document.

Step 4 — Create the Custody Transfer

Create the Custody Transfer from the Stock Picking

Once the stock transfer is in Ready state, click Create Custody Transfer in the action bar. This generates a new custody document already linked to this picking and pre-filled with the operation type, location and product data.

Step 5 — Review Transfer Details

Review Transfer Details and Measurement Method

The custody document opens in Draft state showing Transfer Number, Operation Type, Transfer Date, Approval Policy and linked Stock Transfer. Set the Measurement Method (Manual or Auto SCADA) in the Measurement Conditions section.

Step 5a — Ownership and Transfer Lines

Set Ownership, Demurrage and Transfer Lines

Scroll down to fill the Ownership section (Legal Owner, Custodian, Operator, Carrier), enter Demurrage Hours and Rate, and set API Gravity. The Transfer Lines tab shows the pre-filled product with Planned Qty, Actual Qty, Loss, Gain and Net Standard Volume columns.

Step 5b — Measurement Data Tab

Inspect Measurement Data per Line

Switch to the Measurement Data tab to see the per-line table: Actual Qty, Temperature, Pressure, Volume Correction Factor (VCF), Standard Volume, API Gravity, BSW % and Net Standard Volume. VCF is calculated automatically from the product category's HPC settings.

Step 5c — Parties Tab

Add Multi-Party Records on the Parties Tab

Open the Parties tab to add every stakeholder by role — Buyer, Seller, Operator, Witness, Transporter — each with their partner name and an optional reference. Multiple parties of the same type can be added as separate rows.

Step 6 — Approve the Transfer

Approve the Transfer and Start Physical Movement

Click Approve on the Draft form. The status bar advances to Approved and the action bar now shows Start Transfer, Complete and Raise Dispute. The chatter logs every status transition and operator assignment automatically.

Step 6a — Disputed State

Handle a Disputed Transfer

If a party raises a dispute, the form enters Disputed state with a red warning banner. The action bar offers Resolve Dispute, Cancel and Reset to Draft. The chatter records the full dispute history including who disputed it and when.

Step 7 — Validate the Stock Transfer

Validate Stock Movement from the Linked Picking

Return to the stock transfer. The breadcrumb shows the custody transfer number as a parent and two smart buttons — Moves and Custody Transfer — appear top-right. Click Validate to confirm stock movement; this triggers the Delivery Validation wizard automatically.

Step 8 — Delivery Validation Wizard

Enter Arrival Data in the Delivery Validation Wizard

The wizard captures Arrival Info (Arrival Time, Unloading Start and End Time) and Measurement Values (Temperature, Pressure, API Gravity, Density, BSW %, Sulfur Content) plus actual delivered quantity per line. Click Save and Validate to confirm.

Step 9 — Configure Product Category HPC

Configure HPC Standards via Product Categories

Go to Configuration > Product Categories to set the Hydrocarbon Product Measurement defaults used by the measurement data calculations — standardisation type, API product group and reference conditions per category.

Step 10 — HPC Reference Conditions

Set ASTM Standardisation Type and Reference Conditions

On the product category form, enable HPM Correction and choose the Standardisation Type (e.g. ASTM D1250 / API MPMS 11.1) and API Product Group. Set Reference Conditions (Standard Temperature, Pressure, API Gravity, BSW, Sulfur) used by the VCF calculation for every transfer line in this category.

Step 11 — Completed Transfer

Review the Completed Transfer Measurement Results

On a completed transfer the Measurement Data tab shows the calculated VCF, Standard Volume and Net Standard Volume per line alongside the recorded temperature, pressure and API gravity. The Completion Information section records who completed it and when.

Step 12 — Audit Trail

Monitor All Events in the Global Audit Trail

Open Pipeline & Transport > Audit Trail for a chronological list of every event across all transfers — Created, State Change, Approval, Dispute, Dispute Resolved — each with Event Date, Event Type, User, Description and exact Old Value / New Value.

Create Custody Transfer from Stock Picking

A single button on the stock delivery order generates a linked custody document pre-filled with operation type, location and product data — no manual duplication needed.
Single and Dual Approval Policies

Configure single approval for routine internal moves and dual approval for external or commercial transfers. The second approver must be a different Odoo user from the first.
Delivery Validation Wizard

Captures arrival time, unloading times, actual delivered quantity and on-site measurement values (temperature, pressure, API gravity, BSW, sulfur) before stock is confirmed as done.
Multi-Party Tracking

Add any number of parties — Buyer, Seller, Operator, Witness, Transporter — with role and partner on the Parties tab. Multiple parties of the same role are supported.
HPC Volume Correction (VCF)

Calculates Volume Correction Factor per line using ASTM D1250 or other configured standard. Standard Volume and Net Standard Volume are computed automatically from actual temperature and pressure.
Demurrage Cost Calculation

Enter Demurrage Hours and Rate per hour and the system computes total Demurrage Cost automatically. Helps quantify delay costs without leaving the custody document.
Dispute and Resolution Workflow

Raise a dispute from the Approved state with a mandatory reason. A red banner flags the issue. Resolve with a single action or reset to draft — all steps logged in the chatter.
Global Audit Trail List

A dedicated Audit Trail menu item shows every event across all transfers — state changes, approvals, disputes, resolutions — with event type, user, description and old/new values.
Where does the workflow start — on the custody form or on the stock transfer?
It starts on the stock transfer. Open a delivery order, tick Oil and Gas Transfer, confirm the product lines, then click Create Custody Transfer once the picking reaches Ready state. The system creates the custody document and links it to that picking automatically.
What happens when I click Validate on the stock transfer?
Clicking Validate launches the Delivery Validation wizard, which asks you to record arrival time, unloading times, actual delivered quantities and on-site measurement values. After clicking Save and Validate the stock move is confirmed as Done and the measured data is stored on the custody document's Measurement Data tab.
How is the Volume Correction Factor (VCF) calculated?
VCF is computed automatically per transfer line using the ASTM D1250 standardisation table from the product category's HPC settings. It uses the actual temperature and pressure together with the standard reference conditions defined on the product category. The resulting Standard Volume and Net Standard Volume are displayed on the Measurement Data tab.
Does this module modify or replace Odoo's standard inventory?
No. The custody document sits above stock.picking without inheriting it. Actual inventory moves remain in standard Odoo Inventory. The module adds the Oil and Gas Transfer checkbox and the Create Custody Transfer button to the picking form — it does not alter how stock moves are processed.
