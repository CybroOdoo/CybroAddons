Which accounting standards does this module support?
It is designed to comply with IAS 37 (IFRS) and ASC 410 (US GAAP) standards. It handles present-value capitalization, effective interest method accretion, and books settlement variances as gains/losses.
How is accretion expense generated and posted?
Accretion is calculated using the Effective Interest Method (EIM): Accretion = Current Liability Balance × ((1 + r)^(1/n) - 1). You can choose Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual compounding frequencies. Odoo posts these entries via a daily automated cron job with catch-up functionality.
Can we revise costs or discount rates mid-lifecycle?
Yes. While in the 'Recognized' or 'Executing' states, you can open the ARO Revision Wizard to adjust nominal future costs or discount rates. The system automatically calculates the adjustments, posts the appropriate journal entry, and logs the change in the revision history.
What triggers draft ARO suggestions?
The module includes a certification monitoring cron that runs daily. If any piece of oil equipment passes its certification expiry date, the module automatically generates a draft ARO suggestion to alert managers.
