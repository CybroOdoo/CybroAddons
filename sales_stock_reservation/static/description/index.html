Module Configuration

Under "Inventory" > "Configuration" > "Settings", select the Source Location and Destination Location for stock reservations.

Product Quantity Before Stock Reservation

View product on-hand and forecasted quantities prior to making a stock reservation.

Create Stock Reservation

Users can initiate stock reservation by clicking the "Create stock reservation" button on the sales quotation, which is visible when "Apply stock reservation" is checked.

Stock Reservation Wizard

The wizard displays default quotation lines, allowing users to specify the reserve quantity and select users under "Email Notification" before confirming with "Reserve".

Reserved Stock Details

Users can track created stock reservations and their status from the "Reserved Stock" tab on the sales quotation.

Product Quantity After Stock Reservation

Product forecasted availability is updated immediately to reflect the reserved stock quantities.

Email Notification to Users

Stock users selected in the wizard receive an automated email notification detailing the reserved products.

Cancel Stock Reservation

Clicking the "Cancel stock reservation" button cancels the reservation move and releases the reserved products back into available stock.

Product Forecasted Quantity After Release

Product forecasted quantities are immediately restored upon cancelling the stock reservation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reserve product stock for a sales quotation?

You can reserve stock directly from any quotation in draft state by following these steps:

Steps to Reserve Stock:
  • Open a draft Sales Quotation and enable the Apply stock reservation checkbox.
  • Click the Create stock reservation button that appears in the action header.
  • In the opened wizard, verify the quotation lines, specify the quantity to reserve, and optionally select users to receive an email notification.
  • Click the Reserve button to confirm and create the stock reservation.

Can I reserve partial quantities or select specific order lines?

Yes. When the Stock Reservation wizard opens, it automatically populates all lines from the quotation with their initial quantities. You have the flexibility to edit the Reserve Quantity field on any line to reserve only a fraction of the quotation quantity, or set it to zero for lines you do not wish to reserve.

How does reserving stock affect product inventory and forecasted quantity?

When a stock reservation is confirmed, the module generates confirmed stock moves that move the items from the warehouse stock location to a virtual reservation customer location. This immediately reduces the product's forecasted quantity, ensuring other sales orders and quotations cannot claim the reserved stock, while leaving your physical on-hand quantity intact until actual delivery.

Who receives email notifications when stock is reserved, and what is included?

When a stock reservation is confirmed, an automated confirmation email is sent directly to the customer (if an email address is configured on their contact record). Additionally, users can select internal team members in the Email Notification field of the wizard to receive a copy. Both emails contain a formatted summary table displaying the quotation reference, product names, and reserved quantities.

Where can I track the reserved stock and its status on the quotation?

A dedicated Reserved Stock tab is automatically added to the sales quotation form. It provides full transparency by displaying a detailed list including the reservation reference sequence, order line name, reserved product, reserved quantity, and the real-time status with intuitive color-coded badges (green for Reserved, red for Cancelled).

How do I cancel a stock reservation and release the products?

To cancel a reservation, simply open the quotation and click the Cancel stock reservation button in the header. The system will cancel the underlying stock moves, update the reserved records status to Cancelled, and immediately release the reserved quantities back to your forecasted inventory.

What happens when the sales quotation is confirmed into a Sale Order?

When you click Confirm to turn the quotation into a confirmed Sale Order, the module automatically cancels the quotation stock reservation moves in the background. This avoids duplicate inventory reservations and allows standard Odoo delivery orders (pickings) to handle stock reservation and delivery smoothly.

Can I reserve stock for confirmed or cancelled sales orders?

No. Stock reservation buttons and actions are strictly available when the order is in the Draft (Quotation) stage. This is intentionally designed to protect inventory during customer negotiations before commitment. Once confirmed, Odoo's standard warehouse picking workflow handles order reservation.

Are there any configurations required in Inventory Settings?

The module works out of the box using your quotation's warehouse stock location and standard customer destination location. You can also customize the designated source and destination locations by navigating to Inventory > Configuration > Settings > Stock Reserve Location to suit specific warehouse layouts.

Does this module support multi-company and multi-warehouse environments?

Yes. The reservation process dynamically retrieves the specific company and warehouse associated with the active sales quotation. Stock moves and reserved quantities are assigned to the correct company and warehouse stock locations, preventing cross-company stock conflicts.
