Product Stock Reservation

This module helps sales users reserve product quantities directly from quotations and manage the reservation flow inside Odoo.

Key Highlights

Create stock reservations directly from the sales quotation form.

Release reserved stock when the quotation changes or the reservation must be canceled.

Notify stock users by email during the reservation process from the quotation stage.

Reserve only the required quantity for each quotation line through the reservation wizard.

  • Screenshots
  • Features
  • Released Notes

Configure source and destination locations in Inventory settings for stock reservation.

Review available on-hand quantity before reserving stock from the quotation.

Reserve stock from the sales order when stock reservation is enabled.

Use the wizard to choose quantities, notify users, and confirm the reservation.

Track created reservations from the Reserved Stock smart button on the sale order.

See updated product quantities after a reservation is confirmed.

Send reservation notification emails to the selected stock users.

Cancel an existing reservation to release quantities that were reserved from the quotation.

Confirm that forecasted quantities are restored after the reservation is released.

  • Allow sales users to create product stock reservations from quotations.
  • Cancel reserved stock and release quantities whenever needed.
  • Notify stock users by email during reservation from the quotation stage.
  • Reserve specific quantities through a dedicated reservation wizard.
