Pos Alert in Low Sales Price

To get alert message at the time of validation, when sales price is lower than cost price for corresponding product.

Key Highlights

Gets Alert message

This application is used for getting alert message,when sales price of selected product is lower than its cost price.

Pos Screen

In the pos, Add a product with sales price less than cost price.

Payment Screen

After adding the product, We can go to payment and then on validation,On clicking the validation we can see an alert message.

Payment Validation

After clicking the OK button in alert message, It will continue validation process.

When we click on cancel button in alert message, It will reject or cancel the validation process.

Form View.

To know whether sales price is lower than cost price.

An option to continue validation when clicking the OK button in the alert message.

An option to cancel validation when clicking the cancel button in the alert message.
