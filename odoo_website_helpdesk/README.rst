Website Helpdesk Management
===========================

Let your customers raise support tickets from the website and follow them in
the customer portal, while your helpdesk teams handle, merge and bill the
tickets in the backend.

Key Features
------------

* Ticket form on the website with ticket type, category, products, priority
  and attachments
* Tickets card in the customer portal: search, group, open and download the
  tickets, and chat with the support team
* Email notifications on ticket creation, assignment and stage changes
* Customer rating (1 to 5 stars) and review from the closing email
* Helpdesk teams with a team leader, members, email and project
* Stages with start, closing and cancel flags and an email template
* Merge duplicate tickets into one of them or into a new ticket
* Create project tasks from a ticket and invoice the timesheet hours
* Automatic closing of old tickets
* User, Team Leader and Manager access levels
* Ticket analysis in graph and pivot views
* Works with both Community and Enterprise editions

Installation
------------
No external dependencies. The module depends on Website (``website``),
Sales and Project (``sale_project``), Timesheets (``hr_timesheet``) and
Contacts (``contacts``).

Configuration
-------------
* Settings > Users: give the Helpdesk access level (User, Team Leader or
  Manager) to your support users
* Helpdesk > Configuration > Teams, Stages, Ticket Types, Categories and Tags
* Settings > Helpdesk: enable Create Tasks, Category, Product On Website,
  Helpdesk Menu and Auto Close Ticket, and choose the closing stage and the
  reply template
