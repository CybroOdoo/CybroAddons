Sales Commission
================

A flexible commission system for sales orders in Odoo 20, with rules based on salesperson, partner, product, and discount.


Key Features
------------

* Sales Commission based on Partner, Product and Discount.
* Commission Analysis and Report.
* Odoo 20 Community & Enterprise Edition Support.

Installation
------------
No external dependencies.

Configuration
-------------
Configure sales commission rules under Sales > Configuration > Sales Commission.
