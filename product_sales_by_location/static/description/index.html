Answers Before You Install

Q
Which Odoo editions and deployment environments are supported?
A
Product Sales By Location is fully certified for Odoo 20 Community Edition and also operates flawlessly on Odoo 20 Enterprise and Odoo.sh. Custom third-party modules cannot be installed on Odoo Online (odoo.com SaaS) because Odoo Online does not support external code installation.
 
Q
Where can I configure or select the location on a sales order?
A
Once installed, a new 'Location' column appears directly inside the Sales Order Lines table under Sales → Orders → Quotations / Sales Orders. You can choose any valid internal warehouse location for each line item.
 
Q
How are delivery orders created when lines have different locations?
A
When the sales order is confirmed, Odoo's stock procurement engine groups order lines by their designated source locations and generates separate delivery pickings for each location, enabling warehouse teams to process and validate them independently.
