Commission Plan

Commission Plan window can be accessed from the Configuration menu of the CRM module. Here we can view and create commission plans.

Commission Plan based on Product Category

The commission plan form view allows managing the plan name, duration, and type. You can select the commission plan type as Product-wise or Revenue-based. A product-wise commission plan allows setting commissions based on products or product categories as a percentage or fixed amount.

Revenue-based Commission Plan: Straight Revenue Commission Plan

In revenue-based commission plans, commission is calculated based on the sales order total. When creating a revenue-based commission plan, the calculation type can be chosen as Straight Commission or Graduated Commission. In straight commission, the commission amount type can be configured as a percentage or fixed amount.

Revenue-based Commission Plan: Graduated Revenue Commission Plan

When creating a graduated revenue commission plan, commission rules can be defined for different revenue intervals as a percentage rate or fixed amount.

Assigning Commission Plan for Salesperson

The created commission plan can be assigned to a salesperson. It can be done from the CRM module configuration under the Salespersons menu. Select the user and assign the plan in the Commission Plan field.

Assigning Commission Plan for Sales Team

The created commission plan can be assigned to a sales team. It can be done from the CRM module configuration under the Sales Teams menu. Select the sales team and assign the plan in the Commission Plan field.
In the case of a team-based commission plan, you can choose how the commission is split among team members as Direct or Equal. Direct assigns the commission to the salesperson responsible for the sale order, while Equal divides the commission equally among all team members.

View the Commission Records

The commission records can be viewed from the Sales module under Sales Commissions → Sales Team / Salesperson.

Print the Commission Plan Report in XLSX Format

The CRM Commission Plan module allows administrators and users to print commission reports for any required time period. Reports can be generated in XLSX format. To generate the report, go to the Reporting menu in CRM, select Commission Report, configure the desired criteria, and click Print XLSX.

For creating new commission plans:

  1. Create a new commission plan:

    Create a new commission plan and provide a name to distinguish it from other commission plans.

  2. Select the commission type:

    Product-based: Commission is calculated based on the selected products or product categories. You can configure percentage or fixed-amount commission rules.

    Revenue-based: Commission is calculated based on the revenue generated by the salesperson or sales team.

    Straight revenue: Commission is calculated using a fixed percentage of the eligible revenue. Enter the percentage in the Commission Rate field.

    Graduated revenue: Commission is calculated using different percentage rates for different revenue intervals. Configure the revenue intervals and corresponding percentages in the Graduated Revenue Commission section.

For assigning commission plans:

Go to CRM → Configuration → Salespersons or Sales Teams
  • Open the required Salesperson or Sales Team.
  • Select the required Commission Plan.
  • Save the configuration.

Commission Plan Selection:

When a sale order is confirmed, the system determines the commission plan using the following priority:

  1. If a commission plan is configured on the Sales Team, the Sales Team commission plan is used.
  2. If no commission plan is configured on the Sales Team, the Salesperson's commission plan is used.

The selected commission plan is stored with the commission record so that the commission remains associated with the plan that was used when it was calculated.

Commission Split for Sales Team Plans:

When the commission plan comes from the Sales Team, the Commission Split configured on the Sales Team determines how the calculated commission is distributed.

  • Direct: The entire commission is assigned to the salesperson responsible for the sale order.
  • Equal: The commission is divided equally among all members of the Sales Team.

If the commission plan is taken directly from the Salesperson, the commission is assigned directly to that salesperson and the Sales Team Commission Split is not applied.

When Commission Is Applied:

The system first calculates the commission according to the selected commission plan and its configured rules. The commission is then applied according to the applicable Sales Team or Salesperson workflow.

Commission eligibility follows the configured invoice/payment workflow. Commission is applied only when the required invoice/payment conditions are satisfied.

For viewing Commission Records:

Go to Sales → Sales Commissions → Sales Team / Salesperson
  • View the commissions generated from sale orders.
  • Review the salesperson who received each commission.
  • Identify the Sales Team associated with the commission.
  • View the commission plan and its source (Sales Team or Salesperson).
  • Commission managers and authorized users can access commission information according to their access rights.

For printing the Commission XLSX Report:

Go to CRM → Reporting → Commission Report
  • Select the required commission records or reporting criteria.
  • Generate the XLSX report to analyze the calculated and distributed commissions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the CRM Commission Plan module in Odoo?

The CRM Commission Plan module allows users to create and manage custom commission plans for salespersons and sales teams. It supports both product-based and revenue-based commission calculations with fixed or percentage amounts.

What types of commission plans can I create?

You can create product-based commissions based on product categories, straight revenue commissions with fixed percentages, and graduated revenue commissions calculated at different amount intervals.

Can I export commission reports?

Yes, the module allows you to print Commission Plan Reports in XLSX format. Go to CRM - Reporting - Commission Report, select the required entries, and click Print XLSX.
