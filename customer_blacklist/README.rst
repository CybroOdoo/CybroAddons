Blacklist Customer
==================
This Odoo app helps to blacklist customers. If a customer is blacklisted, a notification is displayed on quotations, sales orders, and customer invoices. In addition, blacklisted customers are prevented from purchasing products from the eCommerce website.

Key Features
------------
* **Easy Partner Blacklisting**: Mark or unmark customers as blacklisted with a single toggle directly in the Contacts form view.
* **Sale Order Warnings**: Real-time warning banners alert sales teams on Quotations and Sales Orders whenever a blacklisted customer is selected.
* **Customer Invoice Alerts**: Dynamic warning notifications alert accounting personnel on Customer Invoices before confirming or posting.
* **eCommerce Purchase Restrictions**: Automatically blocks blacklisted customers from purchasing online by disabling Add to Cart and Buy Now buttons, and hiding wishlist and comparison options.
