Answers Before You Install

Q
What does this module do?
A
Customize available payment providers for all customers (public configuration). Assign specific payment providers for individual customers based on your business needs.
 
Q
Can I use this module with portal customers?
A
Yes. The module works with both internal and portal users. Portal customers will only see the payment methods allowed for them.
 
Q
Can I assign multiple payment providers to one customer?
A
You can assign one or more payment providers to any customer as needed.
