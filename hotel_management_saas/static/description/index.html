Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Booking to Folio & Invoice workflow work?
When a guest makes a reservation, a Booking record is created. Once checked in, a Folio record tracks room charges and extra services. Smart buttons allow generating and viewing customer invoices (account.move) with a single click.
Can I track maintenance and room cleaning tasks?
Yes. The module includes a dedicated Maintenance section where housekeeping or maintenance teams can create work orders per room and track them across customized maintenance stages.
Is this module compatible with Odoo Community and Enterprise?
The Hotel Management module is compatible with Odoo Enterprise only, including Odoo.sh and On-Premise deployments. It is not compatible with Odoo Community Edition.
