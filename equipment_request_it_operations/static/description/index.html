Equipment Request & IT Operation

Employees can send requests to the Department Manager for equipment and for reimbursement of equipment damage expenses.

Key Highlights

Requests for Equipment

Allow your employees to request any equipment from their Department Manager.

Expense and Damage Report

The Department Manager can generate expenses based on the damage details.

Approval from HR

The HR Officer can approve the request and the related expenses.

Internal Transfers for Equipment Requests

The Stock Manager can create internal transfers for equipment requests.

Waiting for HR Approval

After HR Approval

Once HR approves the request, they can approve the associated expenses. The next step is for the Stock Manager to create the internal transfer.
