Theme Aquere
============
A calm blue website theme for a premium packaged drinking water brand: a
floating glass header, a cinematic hero, bento story tiles, an interactive
collection showcase, a sticky process tracker, planet commitments, an enquiry
form and an oversized wordmark footer. Every page is made of editable sections
that can be dropped from the website builder.

Configuration
=============
Install the module, then open the Website app, choose **Themes** and pick
**Theme Aquere**. The Home, Our Story, Our Water, Our Process, Our Planet and
Contact pages and their menus are created on the website.
