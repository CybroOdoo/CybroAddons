Frequently Asked Questions

How does the enrollment and billing process work?
Staff can create an enrollment by selecting the member, membership plan, start date, trainer, fitness class, or personal training package. The system automatically calculates the end date and generates a unique enrollment number. Staff can then create the invoice directly from the enrollment and activate the membership after payment.
Are training sessions generated automatically?
Yes. Once an enrollment becomes active, training sessions are automatically generated up to the end of the current month. A daily scheduled process generates sessions for upcoming months while respecting the membership end date.
What happens when a membership expires?
When the enrollment reaches its end date, it automatically moves to the Expired stage. Staff can then Renew the membership to continue the subscription or Cancel it to stop future session generation.
