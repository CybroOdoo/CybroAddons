Smart Button for Easy Access

A "Send Consolidated Payment Link" button is added directly to the top of the customer(Company) form, providing an intuitive and easily accessible way to trigger the payment process without navigating away from the contact.

Confirmation Wizard

When a user clicks the "Send Consolidated Payment Link" smart button, our module opens a wizard. The sole purpose of this wizard is to ask the user "Are you sure?" before the email is sent. Upon clicking "Send Email," the system immediately sends the pre-defined email template in the background.

Once the email is sent, the corresponding customer will receive it

After a user sends the email, the customer will receive a message containing the payment link. The email includes a "Pay Now" button. When the customer clicks this link, they are taken to a secure payment portal where they can view their outstanding invoice amount and pay the total amount in a single, convenient transaction.

Consolidated Payment Portal

When the customer clicks "Pay Now," they are redirected to a secure payment page. This portal displays the single, consolidated amount of all their outstanding invoices. The customer can then choose a payment method and pay the total balance in one transaction. Once the payment is successful, the system will automatically reconcile it against all the corresponding invoices.

Automatic Invoice Status Update

Once the payment is successful, the system will automatically update the status of all the corresponding invoices to "In Payment".

Handling Multi-Currency Invoices

Even if a customer has outstanding invoices in different currencies, the payment portal simplifies this by presenting a single total. The system will automatically convert all invoice amounts to the company's default currency, allowing the customer to pay one consolidated sum.

One-Click Payment Link

What happens if a customer has no unpaid invoices?

When the customer clicks the "Pay Now" button, the payment portal will display a message like, "All outstanding invoices are settled."

Can I customize the email that gets sent to the customer?

Yes. The module includes a standard email template that you can easily modify through Odoo's developer mode (Settings > Technical > Email Templates).

Will this work with any payment method?

Yes, the payment link will redirect the customer to a portal where they can use any payment providers (like Stripe, PayPal, etc.) that you have configured and published in your Odoo.

What happens if the customer's payment fails?

If the payment is unsuccessful, the invoices will remain in their "Not Paid" state. The customer can simply try to use the payment link again.
