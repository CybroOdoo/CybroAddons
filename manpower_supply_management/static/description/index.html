User Access Setup

Open the user's form in Settings and go to the Access Rights tab. In the Technical section, choose Manage Manpower Supply Management in the Supply Manager dropdown to give the user full access to contracts, workers and skills.

Dashboard Overview

The dashboard gives a quick view of the business. Tiles show the ongoing contracts, the total invoiced amount and the expected amount, while the chart compares contract amounts by day, month or year. The Skills and Available Workers tables show who can be assigned and at what daily rate.

Dashboard Analytics

Scroll down the dashboard for more charts. Contracts are counted by status in a line chart and by customer in a bar chart, worker availability is shown in a doughnut chart, and the Top 10 Customers table lists the number of contracts and the email of each customer.

Create Labour Supply Contract

Go to Operation, then Labour Supply, and create a new contract for a customer. Add a line for each skill needed with the number of labours and the From and To dates. The contract gets its own reference number and starts in the Draft state.

Fetch Available Workers by Skill

Click Fetch and the system assigns available workers who match each required skill. The Workers tab lists their names, phone numbers and rate per day, and the contract moves to the Ready state. If there are fewer workers than requested, an alert is shown on the contract.

Automatic Contract Amount Calculation

The total amount is calculated automatically from the daily rate of each assigned worker and the number of days in the skill period. The contract start and end dates are also taken from the skill lines, so no manual calculation is needed.

Confirm Contract and Create Invoice

After reviewing the workers and the total amount, confirm the contract. A Create Invoice button then appears, and one click generates the customer invoice for the contract amount.

Invoice Smart Button

Once the invoice is created, the contract moves to the Invoiced state. An Invoice smart button at the top of the contract opens the linked invoice, so billing can be tracked from the same screen.

Automatic Contract Expiry

A daily scheduled action marks a contract as Expired when its end date has passed. The assigned workers are made available again, so they can be used in new contracts.

PDF Report Wizard — Customer Filter

Open the Labour Supply Report wizard and choose the Customer wise filter. Select a customer, add a date range if needed, and click Print pdf to generate the report.

Customer-wise PDF Report

The report lists every contract of the selected customer with its reference, start date, end date, total amount and state, ready to print or share.

PDF Report Wizard — State Filter

Choose the State wise filter in the wizard and pick a contract state such as Draft, Ready, Confirmed, Invoiced or Expired. A date range can also be added before printing.

State-wise PDF Report

The report shows only the contracts in the chosen state, with their reference, dates, total amount and state, so management can review each stage separately.

Online Application Form (Website)

Logged-in customers can open Labour Supply on the website and fill in the contract dates. They can add a line for each skill with its dates and quantity, and Submit Request creates a draft contract in the backend.

Labour Supply in My Account

A Labour Supply card appears in the customer's My Account page, giving quick access to their manpower contracts.

Customer Portal Contract List

The portal lists the customer's contracts with the contract number, start date, end date and state, so they can follow progress without contacting the company.

Portal Contract Details

Opening a contract shows its dates, status, customer details and skill requirements. The History section at the bottom keeps the messages related to the contract.

Answers Before You Install

Q
Can I create manpower supply contracts directly in Odoo?
A
Yes. You can create labour supply contracts by defining skills, required workers, duration, and worker rates — and the system will automatically calculate the total contract amount.
 
Q
Is it possible to search and assign workers based on their skills?
A
Yes. Workers can be easily filtered and assigned based on skill categories, ensuring the right workforce is allocated to the right contract.
 
Q
Can I generate invoices from manpower contracts?
A
Absolutely. You can create customer invoices directly from the contract using the Create Invoice button, and track them via a smart button.
