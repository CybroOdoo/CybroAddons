Multiple Payment For Outstanding Dues
=====================================

This module shows the total outstanding due of each customer on the customer
form and lets you register the payment for one or more due invoices at once,
right from the customer.

Key Features
------------

* Shows the total due amount of the customer in the 'Due Statements' smart button.
* Lists every line of the customer's posted, unpaid invoices, grouped by invoice.
* Registers the payment of a single invoice with the 'Pay Now' button.
* Registers the payment of several invoices at once with the 'Pay Due Payment(s)' action.
* Combines the payments of several invoices into one with the 'Group Payments' option.

Installation
------------
No external dependencies.

Configuration
-------------
No additional configuration is required.
