Loyalty Customer Domain
=======================

Restrict the rules of your loyalty, promotion, coupon and discount programs to
the customers you choose. Set a customer domain on a rule, next to its product
conditions: the rule only applies to the sale orders of the customers matching
the domain, and the program is not available to the other customers.

Key Features
------------

* Customer Domain on the conditional rules of the Discount & Loyalty programs
* Any customer condition: tags, country, company, salesperson, specific
  customers...
* Works with the product conditions, minimum quantity and minimum amount of
  the rule
* Customers outside the domain are told the program is not available to them
* Programs without a customer domain keep applying to every customer
* Works with the Community edition

Installation
------------
No external dependencies. The module depends on Sales (``sale_management``)
and Sales Loyalty (``sale_loyalty``).

Configuration
-------------
No additional configuration is required. Open Sales > Promotions > Discount
& Loyalty, and set the Customer Domain on the rules of a program.
