Feature Activation

Activate CAPA & Deviation from Settings

The CAPA & Deviation tier is switched on with a single toggle in the Pharmaceutical ERP configuration - no separate install step. Once enabled, the deviation and CAPA menus and the quality-gating couplings across OOS, IPQC, QC and BMR come online automatically.

OOS Auto-Trigger

OOS Investigation Triggered Automatically

The moment a QC result falls outside its specification's minimum or maximum limit, an Out-of-Specification investigation is raised automatically, with no manual step required from the analyst.

Phase I

Phase I Lab-Error Correction

The initial Phase I review checks for lab or testing errors first, letting a confirmed lab mistake be corrected and closed without escalating into a full investigation.

Phase II

Escalation to Phase II Investigation

When Phase I does not resolve the OOS result, QA escalates it to a Phase II investigation covering the manufacturing process, materials, and equipment involved.

Disposition

Phase II Closure with Release or Reject

Phase II concludes with a documented root-cause finding and a clear release or reject disposition, keeping every OOS outcome fully auditable.

Register

Auto-Created Deviation in the Register

An OOS investigation closed with a Reject disposition, and a signed IPQC failure, each create a corresponding entry in the Deviation Register, so nothing has to be logged twice.

Investigation

Deviation Form & Investigation Kick-Off

Each deviation opens into a structured form capturing classification, description, and investigation ownership from the moment it is raised.

CAPA Link

CAPA Started with Source Deviation Linked

A Corrective & Preventive Action is created directly from its source deviation, preserving a traceable link between root cause and remediation.CAPA records capture the root-cause analysis and an effectiveness check before closure, ensuring corrective actions actually resolved the issue.

Disposition

Deviation Closure with Final Disposition

Deviations close out with a final disposition, giving QA a complete, time-stamped record from detection through resolution.

Quality Gate

One Deviation Flow for QC & IPQC

Deviations from QC tests and from IPQC checks on the BMR follow the same deviation-to-CAPA flow, and open ones on a batch block its step sign-off and BMR completion.

What does this module manage?
It manages the Quality Deviation and Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA) lifecycle within the Pharmaceutical ERP suite.
How does it interact with production?
It automatically blocks BMR sign-offs and QA approvals if there are open deviations linked to the batch.
Can it be uninstalled safely?
Yes, uninstalling returns the OOS, IPQC, and BMR flows to their native behaviour without deviation-based gating.
