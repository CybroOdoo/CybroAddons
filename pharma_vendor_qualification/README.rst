Pharmaceutical ERP — Vendor Qualification
=========================================
Vendor qualification, Approved Vendor List (AVL) and purchase-order AVL enforcement for the Pharmaceutical ERP suite.

Key Features
------------
* Vendor qualification workflow from Draft and Questionnaire Sent through Documents Received and Audit Scheduled to Approved, Not Qualified or Rejected.
* Approved Vendor List (AVL) per product, with approval and expiry dates, vendor item codes and lead times.
* Online portal questionnaire allowing vendors to submit answers and upload supporting documents.
* Configurable audit pass percentage, with a confirmation step before a vendor is marked Not Qualified.
* Purchase order enforcement that blocks ordering a product from a vendor outside its AVL.
* Mail template for issuing questionnaire invitations to vendors.

Installation
------------
No external dependencies.

Configuration
-------------
No additional configuration is required.
