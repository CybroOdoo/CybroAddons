Oil & Gas Royalty
=================
* Oil & Gas Royalty is a specialized solution designed to manage revenue distribution, owner interests, and payment processing efficiently within Odoo.
* It is an open-source module developed to extend Odoo with industry-specific royalty tracking functionalities tailored for oil and gas businesses.
* Oil & Gas Royalty provides integrated features for calculating deductions, managing joint interest billing, and generating statements.
