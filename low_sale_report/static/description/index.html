Answers Before You Install

Q
Can I analyze low sales data for custom date ranges or periods?
A
Yes. You can specify exact start and end dates directly in the report wizard, or configure default intervals (such as last month, last 3, 6, or 12 months) in Sales Settings for routine evaluations.
 
Q
How does the module determine which products qualify as "Low Sale"?
A
The module uses a user-defined Critical Level (Absolute Quantity) threshold. Any product whose total units sold during the analyzed period fall below this threshold is identified and displayed in the report.
 
Q
Can I analyze sales by individual product variants or templates?
A
Yes, both options are supported. You can select By product templates for a broad catalog overview, or choose By product variant to identify specific slow-moving SKUs (such as size, color, or other attributes).
