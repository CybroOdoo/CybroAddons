Employee Stages
===============

Every employee may undergo different stages during their tenure in a company. It may be initial induction (Slap On), probation (Test Period), training (Grounding), employment, etc. The stages may vary according to the organization. It is important to track such stages systematically to assess employee career progression. This module facilitates the management and tracking of different stages throughout an employee's career lifecycle.

Key Features
------------

* Formal career lifecycle progression through distinct stages: **Slap On** (initial joined / onboarding stage), **Grounding** (training), **Test Period** (probation), **Employment** (confirmed), **Notice Period**, and Departure (**Resigned** or **Terminated**).
* Stage history tracking start and end dates with exact duration in days.
* User linking wizard to assign Odoo user credentials dynamically when confirming employment.
* Automatic archiving and inactivation of resigned or terminated employees to safeguard access.
* Prominent stage indicators across Kanban cards, List columns, and interactive Form statusbars.
* Custom stage-based filters and group-by options for rapid reporting and directory segmentation.
