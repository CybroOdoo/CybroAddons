Getting Started with AI Dynamic Dashboard Pro

Unlock the full potential of your data intelligence engine. Learn how to transform complex datasets into actionable visual stories with our enterprise-grade AI toolkit.

Module Workflow

A streamlined 4-step process to deploy your intelligence hub in Odoo.

1

1. Dashboard Registration

Create a new Dashboard menu item in the settings. Specify the parent Odoo menu where your dashboard will appear, and custom client actions will be registered automatically.

2

2. Adding Cards & Components

Add widgets to your dashboard by selecting Odoo models (e.g. Sales, Invoices, Employees). Apply domain filters, select measure fields, and pick your widget type (Chart, KPI Block, Table, or Checklist).

3

3. Interactive Grid & Styling

Arrange cards using our built-in drag-and-drop Gridstack editor. Personalize your workspace with curated color palettes, custom gradients, background images, and custom CSS/JS code.

4

4. AI Auto-Generation & Analysis

Use Google Gemini or Odoo AI to auto-generate a full set of cards based on any Odoo model's fields, or click the AI analyze button to get instant recommendations and insights from card data.
