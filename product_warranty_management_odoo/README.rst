Warranty Management
===================

Manage product warranties in Odoo 20. Set a warranty duration on products,
track the warranty expiry date of every sold product on the sale order, and
let customers submit warranty claims from the website and follow their status
in the customer portal.

Key Features
------------

* Enable a warranty and set its duration in months on any product.
* Sale orders with warranty products show a Warranty smart button after
  confirmation.
* The warranty expiry date of each sold product is calculated from the order
  date and kept on the sale order line.
* Logged-in customers submit warranty claims from the Warranty menu of the
  website, choosing one of their own confirmed orders and a warranty product
  on it.
* A product can have only one open or approved claim per sale order; a new
  claim can be submitted after a rejection.
* Claims are listed under Sales > Orders > Warranty Claims, where they can be
  approved or rejected, or reset to draft to change the decision.
* Customers follow their claims and their status from the My Claims card of
  the customer portal.

Installation
------------
No external dependencies.

Configuration
-------------
No additional configuration is required.
