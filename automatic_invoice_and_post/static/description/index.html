Answers Before You Install

Q
What does this module do?
A
It automatically generates and posts a customer invoice when a delivery order is validated, and optionally sends it to the customer by email — removing the need for manual invoicing.
 
Q
Will the invoice email be sent to the customer automatically?
A
Yes. Once the delivery is validated and the invoice is generated (and posted), the module can automatically send the invoice by email to the customer.
 
Q
Does the invoice get created automatically, or do I still need to confirm it manually?
A
The invoice is created automatically the moment the delivery is marked as done. If the auto-post option is enabled in settings, it's also posted automatically with no manual confirmation needed.
