Answers Before You Install

Q
How can cashiers view customer images in POS?
A
Cashiers can view customer profile pictures directly in the customer selection list when choosing a partner in a POS session.
 
Q
Are customer tags displayed in POS customer selection?
A
Yes, all category tags assigned to a customer in the Contacts app are automatically displayed in the POS partner line.
 
Q
Does this module require extra configuration?
A
No extra setup is required. Once installed, customer images and tags are automatically loaded into POS sessions.
