Theme Nutivo

Key Features

Complete Store Design

An ivory, forest green and gold design with an announcement bar, header, footer and ready-made Home, Our Story and Contact pages.

Shop Mega Menu

The Shop menu lists up to four categories with their products, and a gifting feature card. It is a regular mega menu that you edit in the website builder.

Product Cards With Pack Sizes

Cards show the variants of a product as size chips, a +N popover for the rest, an Add button, ratings and ribbons.

Quick View

Open any product in a popup with all its options, add-ons and custom text, then add it to the bag without leaving the page.

Bag And Wishlist Drawers

Side drawers with a free shipping meter, suggested products and the standard eCommerce cart and wishlist data.

Shop Filters

The standard eCommerce shop with price, rating and highlight filters, category rail, sorting and a grid or list switch.

Editable Snippets

Twenty-one Nutivo blocks in the website builder: hero, categories, bestsellers, hampers, festive offers, testimonials, newsletter, FAQ and more.

Smooth Motion

Smooth scrolling and scroll animations that respect the reduced motion setting of the visitor.

Responsive And Accessible

Built for phone, tablet and desktop, with keyboard friendly drawers and labelled icons.
