When does the Inspect button appear?
The Inspect button becomes active on manufacturing orders once they reach the "Done" state and satisfy the inspection point criteria configured for the product.
How are inspection orders created?
They are automatically generated from the manufacturing order using the predefined checklist criteria from the relevant inspection points.
