Multi Barcode for Products

This module allows you to create multiple product barcodes for Sales, Purchase, Inventory, and Invoicing.

Email Us WhatsApp
Variant Multi Barcodes Sales & Purchases Delivery Operations Customer Invoicing

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Multiple Barcodes per Variant

Easily assign multiple alternate barcodes for product variants, supporting different packaging sizes and supplier codes.

 

Instant Search by Barcode

Quickly find and filter products in list and kanban views by typing or scanning any configured alternate barcode.

 

Sales Order Line Scanning

Scan or enter barcodes directly in Sales Order lines to immediately resolve products and autofill description and pricing.

 

Purchase Order Line Scanning

Accelerate vendor orders by scanning supplier or packaging barcodes directly into Purchase Order lines.

 

Delivery & Transfer Operations

Scan barcodes directly in delivery orders and inventory moves to quickly verify items and speed up warehouse fulfillment.

 

Customer Invoicing & Bills

Scan or enter multi-barcodes in Customer Invoices or Vendor Bills to automatically set the line product, account, and taxes.

Q
What does the "Multi Barcode for Products" module do in Odoo?
A
The Multi Barcode for Products module allows each product to have multiple barcodes instead of a single default one.
