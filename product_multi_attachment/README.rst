Product Multi Attachment
========================

The Product Multi Attachment module allows users to attach multiple documents, manuals, and images directly to products in Odoo 20. It provides seamless visibility of product attachments in Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, and automatically attaches them to outgoing emails.

Key Features
------------

* Add multiple attachments to products and product variants.
* Convenient attachment management under the General Information tab of the product form.
* View product attachments directly within Sale Order lines.
* View product attachments directly within Purchase Order lines.
* Automatically include product attachments in outgoing emails when sending quotations or RFQs.
* Supports diverse file formats including PDFs, images, documents, and spreadsheets.
* Seamless synchronization between product records and transactional order lines.

Installation
------------
No external dependencies are required.

Configuration
-------------
No additional configuration is required.
