Frequently Asked Questions

Q
What types of attachments can I add to products?
A
You can add various file types, including images (JPEG, PNG), documents (PDF, Word), and spreadsheets, among others.
Q
Can I view attachments directly in sales and purchase orders?
A
Yes, attachments linked to a product will be visible directly in the sales order and purchase order lines, making it easy to access relevant documents without navigating away.
Q
Are attachments automatically added when sending emails?
A
Yes, when you email quotations or RFQs to customers or vendors, all attachments linked to the products on the order lines are automatically included in the email.
