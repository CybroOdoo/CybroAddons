Product Brand Management for odoo online
========================================
A comprehensive Product Brand Management application for Odoo Online 19.4
that simplifies the management of Brands, Brand Categories, and Brand Tags.
It enhances product organization with custom dashboards, reports, filters,
and an intuitive user experience for efficient brand management.
