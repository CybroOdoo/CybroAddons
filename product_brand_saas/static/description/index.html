Frequently Asked Questions

How do I associate a product with a brand?
Open any product template or variant form (e.g. in the Inventory app), and you will find a new Brand dropdown field. Select the desired brand to assign it. You can also view and edit associated products directly from the Brand form lines.
Can I customize lifecycle stages for brands?
Yes. Go to the Brand Configuration menu and select Brand Stages. Here you can define, sort, and rename stages (such as Draft, Active, Discontinued) to track your brand management process visually in Kanban.
Is this module compatible with Odoo Online (SaaS)?
Yes, this module is built specifically for compatibility with Odoo Online (SaaS) and Odoo Enterprise (Odoo.sh & On-Premise) without requiring backend system packages.
