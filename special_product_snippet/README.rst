Special Product Website Snippet
===============================

Special Product Website Snippet allows website administrators to highlight and feature individual products on any webpage using Odoo's native Website Builder. With direct product selection and multiple modern layout templates, you can create engaging product showcases in seconds.

Key Features
------------
* **Drag-and-Drop Building Block**: Place the Special Product snippet anywhere on your website, landing pages, or homepage using the standard Odoo Website Builder.
* **Direct Product Record Selector**: Choose any product from your catalog with an intuitive Many2One search and autocompletion selector in the sidebar options panel.
* **4 Dynamic Presentation Templates**:
    * **First (Hero Split Layout)**: Side-by-side product imagery and details with prominent action buttons.
    * **Second (Centered Showcase)**: Centered product image display with balanced typography and pricing.
    * **Third (Framed Product Card)**: Structured card with a distinct top header, centered image, and full-width CTA.
    * **Fourth (Minimalist Modern Card)**: Clean, elevated card design with subtle shadows and a modern pill button.
* **Live Store Synchronization**: Dynamically fetches product titles, high-resolution images, and prices formatted in active website currencies in real time.
* **Direct eCommerce Link**: Integrated "View" call-to-action button automatically links visitors straight to the product details page in the online store (``/shop/...``) to view inventory and complete checkout.
* **Multi-Snippet Support**: Add multiple product snippets to the same webpage, each featuring different products and unique layout templates.
* **Fully Responsive Layout**: Built with fluid CSS Flexbox to ensure perfect rendering across mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop monitors.
* **Native Odoo 20 Architecture**: Implemented with Owl ``BaseOptionComponent`` and editor-safe lifecycles to guarantee clean, robust website builder integration.

Installation
------------
No external dependencies.

Configuration
-------------
1. Go to the **Website** app and click **Edit** to open the Website Builder.
2. Drag and drop the **Special Snippet** block from the snippet panel onto your page.
3. In the sidebar options panel, choose your desired **Product** from the record selector.
4. Select one of the 4 available **Template** layouts.
5. Click **Save** to publish your special product showcase.
