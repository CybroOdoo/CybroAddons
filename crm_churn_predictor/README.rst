CRM Churn Predictor
===================
* Smart Risk Scoring: Calculates churn risk on a 0-100 scale using real business data like purchase recency, revenue trends, and support burden.
* Automated Alerts: Instantly notifies Account Managers by scheduling follow-up tasks and leaving warning notes when a customer's risk level rises to High or Critical.
* Self-Healing Scores: Automatically lowers risk levels when a customer makes a new purchase or their support situation improves.
* Retention Recommendations: Provides smart, actionable win-back strategies tailored to the customer's specific failing points.
* Risk Kanban Board: Visually manage your customer retention pipeline by grouping customers into Low, Medium, High, and Critical risk stages.
* Role-Based Access Control: Secure customer data by restricting access so sales representatives only track their own customers, while managers oversee the entire company.

Configuration
============
- Navigate to **Settings > Users & Companies > Users**.
- Locate the **CRM Churn Predictor** field and set the access right to either "User: Own Customers" or "Manager: All Customers".
