Key Highlights

Trust → Site → Department Hierarchy

Adds full operational depth beneath each Trust: sites containing departments, all cascade-linked and security-scoped.

CQC Inspection Management

Records the 5 KLOE ratings (Safe / Effective / Caring / Responsive / Well-Led) plus Overall, with full inspection history and attachments.

Live CQC Rating on Trust

Each Trust's latest CQC rating and date are auto-pulled from its most recent inspection and shown for filtering and grouping.

Auto-Computed Bed Capacity

Total beds sum automatically across all sites, with an optional manual override for definitive external figures.

England & Scotland Aware

CQC fields apply to England; switching to Scotland auto-sets registration to "not applicable" (Scotland uses Healthcare Improvement Scotland).
