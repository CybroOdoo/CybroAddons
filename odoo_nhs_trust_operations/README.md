# NHS Trust Management — Operations & Compliance

Extends the NHS Trust register with the operational depth Trust corporate
teams need every day — every hospital site, every department, every CQC
inspection and the financial picture that goes with them.

## Why this module

Once your Trust register is in place, the next questions follow within hours:
"Which sites does each Trust operate?", "How many beds?", "What's the CQC
rating across our portfolio?", "What's the financial position?" Operations &
Compliance answers all of those inside Odoo, without leaving the Trust record.

## Key features

**Multi-site estate** — capture every hospital, clinic, ambulance station
  and admin building with GPS coordinates, A&E classification (Type 1–4),
  bed capacity, ICU beds, operating theatres, opening hours and clinical
  specialties.
**Department-level detail** — drill below site into clinical, corporate,
  support and research departments with heads, staff counts and specialty
  links.
**CQC inspection management** — full inspection history with the five Key
  Lines of Enquiry (Safe, Effective, Caring, Responsive, Well-Led), overall
  rating, inspector details, inspection type (comprehensive / focused /
  responsive / thematic / follow-up) and CQC report attachments.
**Calendar of upcoming inspections** — see what's coming due across your
  estate in one place.
**Financial oversight** — annual budget, income, expenditure, automatically
  computed surplus/deficit (coloured by sign), capital allocation and PFI
  obligations.
**Workforce & capacity** — total FTE workforce and bed capacity, with
  auto-aggregation from sites and manual override capability.
**Clinical specialty register** — configurable lookup of NHS-coded
  specialties for sites and departments.
**Inherited Trust form** — adds Sites, CQC Inspections, Financials and
  Workforce tabs directly to the Trust form. Stat buttons for quick drill-in.

## What ships in this module

| New models | 4 (Site, Specialty, Department, CQC Inspection) |
| Extensions to nhs.trust | 14 additional fields, 4 computed values |
| Inherited views | Trust form extended with 4 new tabs + 3 stat buttons |

## Compliance ready

The CQC inspection model captures the data needed to evidence compliance with
the Care Quality Commission's regulatory framework and integrates with the
audit chatter on each Trust — every inspection is fully traceable.

## Compatibility

Requires NHS Trust Management — Core
Compatible with Reports & Documents for branded PDF output
