Odoo Contact Approval
=====================
This module helps to manage contacts through the Approval manager's validation process.

Key Features
------------

* Only authorized users can approve contacts
* Multiple contacts can be approved or cancelled with just one click.
* Users can only use approved contacts in Sales, Purchase, Inventory, and Invoice models.
* Filter contacts based on their approval state.
* Support in Community, Enterprise & Odoo.sh

Installation
------------
* Depends on: Contacts (contacts), Sales Management (sale_management), Purchase (purchase), Inventory (stock), and Invoicing (account).
