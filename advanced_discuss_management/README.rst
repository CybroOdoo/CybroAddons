Advanced Discuss Management
===========================

Advanced Discuss Management turns the Odoo 20 Discuss app into a board. When no
conversation is open, the Chats, Channels and Meetings tabs of the sidebar show
their conversations as Kanban cards, threads are listed right below their parent
channel, and a video meeting can be started in one click.

Key Features
------------

* Kanban board of chats: direct and group chats shown as cards with avatar, last
  message and unread counter.
* Kanban board of channels: channels and their threads shown as cards, with the
  parent channel on each thread card.
* Meetings board: planned and past video meetings shown as cards.
* Filters and search: the board follows the filter chips (Unread, Group, Thread,
  Today) and the search box of the sidebar.
* Threads under their channel: in the sidebar, each thread is indented right below
  its parent channel.
* One-click meeting: a Meeting button on every board starts a video meeting.
* Camera access check: a clear message is shown when the browser blocks the camera
  or the page is not served over HTTPS.
