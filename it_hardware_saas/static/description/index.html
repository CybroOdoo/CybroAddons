Frequently Asked Questions

What types of IT hardware products are included?
The module supports a comprehensive catalog including laptops (Dell Latitude, ThinkPad X1 Nano), desktops, servers, CCTV cameras, keyboards, and mice. Each product is configured with relevant hardware attributes like Processor, Memory, Hard Drive, OS, and more.
Does this module include an eCommerce storefront?
Yes. The module integrates with Odoo Website Sale to provide a full online store for IT hardware. Customers can browse products with real-time stock availability, submit CRM inquiries, and place orders directly through the website.
How does the helpdesk and repair workflow function?
A dedicated Customer Support helpdesk team is pre-configured with product returns and repair capabilities enabled. Customers can submit tickets via the website, and support agents can initiate product returns or create repair orders directly from the ticket.
Can I track serial numbers for hardware products?
Yes. All storable hardware products are configured with serial number tracking. Serial/lot numbers are displayed on delivery slips and invoices, and the module enables multi-location stock management for complete inventory visibility.
Is clean installation supported without demo records?
Yes. The module installs cleanly without dummy demo records, allowing you to configure production IT hardware products, projects, and field services directly.
