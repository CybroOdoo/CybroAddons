Total Items and Total Quantity in POS
=====================================
This module shows the total items and total quantities of products, along with the total amount, on the POS screen and on the bill.

Installation
============
    - https://www.odoo.com/documentation/20.0/setup/install.html
    - Install our custom addon

Configuration
=============
* To show the total items and quantities on the POS screen, the user needs to
  enable 'Enable Total Items' under the PoS Interface section.
* To show the total items and quantities on the bill, the user needs to
  enable 'Enable Total Quantity' under the Bills & Receipts section.
