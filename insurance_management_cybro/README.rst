Insurance Management
====================

This module manages insurance policies, customer insurances, claims and agent
salaries or commissions.

Key Features
------------

* Manage insurance policies, policy types and payment types.
* Issue insurances to customers and invoice them in full (fixed payment) or
  in monthly installments.
* Register claims on confirmed insurances and generate settlement vendor
  bills.
* Pay agents a fixed salary, a commission, or both. The commission rate is
  set on each insurance.
* Two security groups, Insurance User and Insurance Manager, and multi-company
  support.

Installation
------------

No external dependencies. The module depends on the Odoo Invoicing app
(``account``).

Configuration
-------------

* Assign the users to the *Insurance User* or *Insurance Manager* group
  (Settings > Users). The manager manages the master data, the agents and
  their salaries, and implies the Invoicing access right.
* Invoices, claim bills and salary bills can only be generated by users who
  have the Invoicing access right.
* Before creating insurances, set up the master data under
  Insurance > Configuration (policies, policy types and payment types) and
  add the agents. The agent must be linked to a user to be paid.

Usage
-----

1. Go to **Insurance > Insurance Management > Insurance** to create a new customer insurance.
2. Select the Customer, Policy, Payment Type, Policy Number, Policy Duration, and Agent.
3. Click **Confirm** to validate the insurance policy.
4. Click **Create Invoice** to generate customer invoices for fixed or monthly installment payments.
5. In case of claims, go to **Insurance > Insurance Management > Claims** to register claims and generate settlement bills.
6. To pay agent commissions or salaries, open the Agent record and click **Salary Payment**.
