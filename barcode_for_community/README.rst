Barcode
==============================
This module enables barcode scanning for inventory adjustments and stock transfers in Odoo Community Edition. It supports scanning products, lots, serial numbers, locations, and packages directly from a camera or barcode scanner, and automatically creates or updates stock move lines in real time. Batch transfers and printable barcode PDFs for products, locations, and operation types are also supported.

=============
