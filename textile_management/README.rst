Textile Management
==================

Run the textile business in one app: textile products, sale and purchase
orders, bills of materials and manufacturing orders, with a customer and vendor
report of the textile orders, a website inquiry form and customer feedback at
checkout.

Key Features
------------

* Textile Management app grouping textile products, sale orders, purchase
  orders, bills of materials and manufacturing orders
* Records created from the app are flagged as textile, as are website orders
  of textile products and the manufacturing orders created on order for
  textile sale orders
* Textile sale and purchase orders (lines and catalog), bills of materials and
  manufacturing orders only offer textile products
* The Replenish on Order (MTO) route is activated on install
* Customer and vendor PDF reports of the textile orders, for any period, with
  amounts in the order currency
* Website inquiry form with a thank-you page, and backend inquiry management
  with Draft, Verified and Replied stages
* Customer feedback on the payment step of the checkout: a 1 to 4 star rating
  and a comment, shown in the Customer Feedback tab of the website order
* Works with both Community and Enterprise editions

Installation
------------
No external dependencies. The module depends on ``web``, ``mail``, ``website``,
``stock``, ``mrp``, Sales (``sale_management``), Purchase (``purchase``),
``sale_mrp`` and eCommerce (``website_sale``).

Configuration
-------------
No additional configuration is required. Mark products as Textile Product, or
create them from the Textile Management menu.

Each user sees the Textile Management menus of their Sales, Purchase and
Manufacturing access. Sales users process the website inquiries of their
companies.
