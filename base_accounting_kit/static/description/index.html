1. All Financial Reports, One Menu

All dynamic financial reports are now available in the Reporting menu of Odoo 20 Community: P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, General Ledger, Trial Balance, Partner Ledger, Aged Receivable and Payable, Tax Report, daily books and management reports.

2. Profit and Loss

Income, cost of revenue and expenses down to every account: unfold all lines in one click, compare with previous periods or the same period last year, show it by month or quarter, and include draft entries or debit and credit columns.

3. Balance Sheet

Assets, liabilities and equity at any date, down to every account: unfold all lines in one click, compare with earlier dates, filter by journal and include draft entries or debit and credit columns.

4. General Ledger

Every account with its journal items, initial balance, debit, credit and running balance per fiscal year: filter by journal, account, partner or analytic account, unfold all and search any line.

5. Trial Balance

Initial balance, debit, credit and end balance of every account for any period, with result brought forward; unfold an account to see the journal items behind it and export to PDF or Excel.

6. Partner Ledger

Customer and vendor balances with every invoice, payment and credit note behind them, an initial balance per partner, and filters for receivable or payable accounts and reconciled entries.

7. Aged Receivable

Open customer balances spread over Not Due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91–120 and older than 120 days, with every open invoice per customer, as of today or any date and with your own period length.

8. Aged Payable

What you owe each vendor by age bucket, with every open bill and payment behind the total, as of today or any date, to plan payments and spot overdue bills at a glance.

9. Tax Report

Sales and purchase taxes for the period with the net and tax amount of every tax rate, posted or including draft entries, matching the journal items behind every figure.

10. Journals Audit

Every journal with its debit and credit totals for the period; unfold a journal to check each entry line by line, sorted by date or by journal entry number.

11. Bank Reconciliation Report

For each bank account: the balance as per bank statement, receipts and payments not yet on the statement, the adjusted balances and the difference, as of any date.

12. Day Book

All journal entries day by day with their debit, credit and balance; unfold a day to see every entry, filtered by journal and account.

13. Cash Book

Petty cash movements with initial balance, debit, credit and running balance: every receipt, expense and transfer listed line by line for the period.

14. Bank Book

Every bank account with its initial and running balance; unfold to see each deposit, payment and transfer, filtered by journal and account.

15. Bank Reconciliation Widget

Bank lines are grouped as Sure match, Please confirm and Needs you. Accept every sure match in one click, open a line to see the suggested invoices and the entry before posting, book the rest to an account or a model, in cards or a list.

16. Open Items Matching

Match invoices with payments and credit notes per partner, no bank line needed. Sure matches are proposed, early-payment discounts are applied, and any difference stays open, is written off or goes to a model. Works for receivable, payable and other reconcilable accounts.

17. Bank Statement Import

Import CAMT, MT940, CSV, Excel, OFX and QIF files from the bank journal. For a CSV or Excel file from any bank, say once what each column holds and save it as the format of the journal; lines already imported are skipped.

18. Fiscal Year and Year-end Closing

Define fiscal years and close them with checks first: earlier years closed, bank reconciled, no drafts, depreciation and recurring invoices done. The result goes to retained earnings with a closing entry and the year is locked; reopen if needed.

19. Month-end Closing

A checklist for every month from your own task templates, each task with a responsible person and a due date. Checks for drafts, unreconciled bank lines, payments in transit and duplicate bills come before the month is closed and locked.

20. Assets and Depreciation

Assets with their model, accounts and a full depreciation board, posted month by month. Modify Value raises or lowers the value with an entry, Sell or Dispose closes the asset and posts the gain or loss, and assets can be paused and resumed.

21. Customer Follow-ups

Follow-up levels by days overdue, each with its own email or letter. The Follow-up Reports list every customer who needs action, and each customer page shows the overdue invoices ready to send or print.

22. Post-dated Cheques and Check Printing

Receive and issue post-dated cheques with a cheque reference and an effective date, and print checks for vendor payments straight from the payment.

23. Recurring Invoices and Entries

Customer invoices, vendor bills and journal entries repeated on a schedule: every so many days, weeks, months or years, posted automatically or left as drafts.

24. Customer Credit Limit

Set a warning amount and a blocking amount on any customer: you are warned when the customer goes over the first and stopped at the second.

25. Bulk Payments

Pay many vendor bills or collect many invoices in one go, from one bank journal, with one total and a printable summary.

26. Multiple Invoice Copies

Name the copies of each sales or purchase journal, such as Original, Duplicate and Triplicate, and choose how the copy name is shown on the printed invoice.

27. Foreign Currency Revaluation

Revalue open foreign-currency balances at a date: unrealized gains and losses are posted to their own accounts and reversed automatically the next day.

28. Lock Dates

Lock all entries, entries with taxes, invoices or bills up to a date, with a final hard lock that only moves forward.

29. To Review Menu

One menu for everything that needs a look: entries to review, draft entries, bank transactions and open items, next to journal items and the audit trail.

Before Installation

This module uses a few external Python packages. Install them first, then install the module.

Required packages: openpyxl, ofxparse, qifparse and xlsxwriter. Install these before installing the module using the commands below.
Install the Python packages
bash
pip install openpyxl
pip install ofxparse
pip install qifparse
pip install xlsxwriter

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the Accounting menu missing in Odoo 20 Community?
Odoo Community Edition does not include the full Accounting app — it is an Enterprise feature. This kit restores financial reports, assets, budgets and bank reconciliation to Community.
The Accounting menu is not showing after install — how do I fix it?
Restart the Odoo service, hard-refresh your browser, and confirm the module status shows Installed (not just Downloaded). The Accounting menu then appears in the top navigation bar.
Is the Full Accounting Kit free?
Yes. The kit is free for Odoo 20 Community Edition, with no per-user fees and no subscription.
Which financial reports are included?
Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, General Ledger, Trial Balance, Partner Ledger, Aged Receivable, Aged Payable, Cash Flow Statement, Tax Report and Journal Audit — all exportable to PDF and XLSX.
Does it work on Odoo Online and Odoo.sh?
No, it will work only on Community Edition.
How do I get a Profit and Loss report in Odoo 20 Community?
Install the kit and open Reporting › Profit and Loss. It shows income, cost of revenue and expenses for any period, live on screen: unfold every account, drill down to the journal entry, compare with earlier periods, and export to PDF or Excel.
Is there a Balance Sheet report for Odoo 20 Community?
Yes. Reporting › Balance Sheet shows assets, liabilities and equity as of any date, with comparison columns, drill-down and PDF / Excel export.
Does the kit add a General Ledger and Trial Balance to Community?
Yes. The General Ledger shows every account with its opening balance and moves; the Trial Balance shows opening, period and closing balances per account. Both filter by journal, analytic account and partner.
Can I see Aged Receivable and Aged Payable in Odoo 20 Community?
Yes. Both reports list overdue amounts per partner in ageing buckets, and you can open the invoice behind any amount.
Is there a Cash Flow Statement and a Tax Report?
Yes. The Cash Flow Statement splits cash into operating, investing and financing activities, and the Tax Report shows the tax base and tax amount per tax for the period.
How do I reconcile bank statements in Odoo 20 Community?
Open Review › Bank Transactions. The reconciliation widget groups the bank lines as Sure match, Please confirm and Needs you; accept every sure match in one click, check the suggestions, and book the rest to an account or a model.
Can I import bank statements in Odoo 20 Community?
Yes. Use Import on the bank journal card for CAMT, MT940, CSV, Excel, OFX and QIF files. For a CSV or Excel file from any bank, set the columns once and save them as the format of the journal.
Does it support asset management and depreciation in Community?
Yes. Assets can be created from vendor bills, depreciated linear or degressive with a full depreciation board, revalued, paused and sold or disposed.
Can I close a fiscal year or a month in Odoo 20 Community?
Yes. Year-end Closing runs its checks, posts the result to retained earnings and locks the year (it can be reopened). Month-end Closing gives each month a task checklist with responsible people before the month is locked.
Does it include customer follow-ups, recurring invoices and post-dated cheques?
Yes. Follow-up levels with reminder emails and letters, recurring invoices, bills and journal entries, post-dated cheques, check printing and bulk payments are all included.
Will it work alongside Odoo Enterprise Accounting?
No. The kit is made for Odoo 20 Community Edition, which does not include Enterprise Accounting; it is not meant for Enterprise databases.

Docker Users

Running Odoo in Docker? Install the qifparse package safely with the steps below.

1Create a custom Dockerfile (e.g. Dockerfile.odoo) with this content:
Dockerfile.odoo
FROM odoo:20.0
USER root
# Install qifparse to a custom directory
RUN pip3 install --target=/opt/qiflibs qifparse
# Add that directory to PYTHONPATH
ENV PYTHONPATH="/opt/qiflibs:$PYTHONPATH"
USER odoo
2Point docker-compose.yml at that Dockerfile for the web service:
docker-compose.yml
services:
  web:
    build:
      context: .
      dockerfile: Dockerfile.odoo
3Rebuild and restart the containers:
bash 
docker-compose build
docker-compose up -d
