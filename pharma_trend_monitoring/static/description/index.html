Settings

Minimum Results and Range Width in Settings

Settings hold the number of approved results needed before a range can be approved (two by default) and the standard-deviation multiple used to propose it.

Watch

Watch a Quantitative Parameter on the Specification

Ticking Watch on a specification line starts trend monitoring for that parameter. Only quantitative parameters can be watched.

Range Approval

QA Approves the Normal Range

Once enough approved results exist, QA approves the normal range. The chatter records each old and new range, the results it was built from and who approved it.

Flagged Result

Out-of-Trend Result Flagged on the Test Order

A result outside the range is flagged beside the result, against the range in force at the time. A later re-approval never changes the flag of results already evaluated.

Batch Hold

Batch Held Until QA Review

The test order cannot be approved until QA writes a note and ticks it reviewed. A filter on the test order list shows every flagged order.

Trend Chart

Trend Chart with the Normal Range

Recent results are plotted with the approved range drawn on the chart, so drift is visible before it becomes an out-of-specification result.

APQR Export

APQR Trending Export to XLSX

Per product and period, the export lists results, mean and standard deviation, the range that applied, the flagged count and a chart. It covers the trending section only.

Normal Range Control

Re-approvable ranges with a per-result snapshot of the range used.

OOT Flagging

Flags on the test order with a mandatory QA review note.

Trend Chart

Recent results against the approved range.

APQR Export

An XLSX of the trending section for the annual product review.
Is an OOT result the same as OOS?
No. OOS is outside the specification. OOT is outside the normal range QA approved for the parameter, which sits inside the specification and gives early warning.
Does re-approving a range change earlier flags?
No. Each result keeps a snapshot of the range in force when it was evaluated.
Does it cover stability trending or the whole APQR?
No. Stability trending is out of scope, and the export is only the trending section of the APQR, not batches, yields, deviations, CAPAs, change controls, complaints or returns.
