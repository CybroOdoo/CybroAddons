Feature Activation

Activate Equipment Qualification & Maintenance from Settings

The equipment tier is switched on with one toggle in the Pharmaceutical ERP configuration. The Equipment menu, the fitness check and the dashboard figures come online with it.

Categories

Equipment Categories Carry the Class and GMP Defaults

Each category sets the equipment class (testing instrument, manufacturing equipment or utility system) and whether its equipment is GMP critical. Sterile manufacturing categories are flagged here.

Register

Equipment Register with Live Status Badges

Every item has a unique equipment code per company and shows its qualification, calibration, cleaning and sterile state at a glance, plus a warning while a breakdown repair is open.

Qualification

Qualification Tab: Protocol, IQ / OQ / PQ and Dates

The qualification protocol, qualified-on date and requalification date sit together. The IQ, OQ and PQ flags are driven by the signed records once they exist.

Qualification Records

IQ, OQ and PQ Records Created on Registration

Registering GMP critical equipment generates its three qualification records, the way SOP approval generates training records. They are listed with their status, report and sign-off.

Sign-off

QA Signs Off Each Stage in Order

QA records the performed date and report reference and passes or fails the stage. OQ cannot pass before IQ, nor PQ before OQ, and a failed stage needs written findings.

Requalification

Requalify Equipment Right Away

A move, a major repair or a change of use calls for requalification now. QA records the protocol and reason, renews the qualified date and schedules the next one.

Calibration

Calibration Record with As-Found and As-Left Results

Each calibration is its own record: enter the as-found and as-left reading per test point and the tolerance decides pass or fail. The interval and reminder carry over from the previous calibration, and QA verification updates the instrument and opens an impact assessment when it was found out of tolerance.

Maintenance

Requests Move New, In Progress, Completed, QA Verified

Preventive, breakdown and cleaning requests follow one GMP workflow. The person who completed the work cannot verify it, and verified work cannot be cancelled.

Cleaning

Clean Hold, Dirty Hold and Changeover

Each cleaning sets how long the equipment stays clean while unused (clean hold) and how long it may wait dirty after use (dirty hold), carried over to the next cleaning. A cleaning started after the dirty hold is flagged for QA, and the last product that ran makes a changeover cleaning required where equipment is not dedicated.

Dashboard

Dashboard Figures and Due Lists

Four figures and the Calibration Due, Requalification Due and Awaiting QA Verification worklists show what needs attention. Reminders are raised ahead of the due dates.

Manufacturing Order

Equipment Fitness Check on Order Confirmation

Confirming a manufacturing order checks the equipment behind its work centres. Unfit equipment warns or blocks, and a QA override with a justification records the decision.

QC Results

Instrument and Calibration Position on Every Result

Each QC result records the instrument used and whether it was in calibration at the moment of measurement. A failed calibration can be assessed for impacted results and batches.

Equipment Register

Testing instruments, manufacturing equipment and utility systems, each with a unique code, class and GMP critical flag.

Qualification Records

IQ, OQ and PQ records generated at registration, signed off in order, with requalification scheduled.

Calibration & Cleaning

Calibration results and certificates, clean and dirty hold, changeover and optional sterile manufacturing.

Fitness Gating

Off, warn or block on manufacturing orders and QC results, with a documented QA override.
When are the IQ, OQ and PQ records created?
When GMP critical equipment is registered, or when an existing item is marked GMP critical. Stages already ticked on the register are carried over as passed.
Who can sign off a qualification stage?
Only QA managers. Each stage needs a performed date and report reference, and must follow the previous stage. Signed records are GMP records and can be archived but not deleted.
Does it need the Enterprise maintenance planner?
No. It builds on the Community maintenance app and keeps its own link between equipment and work centres. Work-centre equipment from the Enterprise planner is checked too when present.
Can the equipment check be relaxed?
Yes. The check can be set to off, warn or block in Settings. A QA override with a justification lets a blocked order proceed and is recorded.
