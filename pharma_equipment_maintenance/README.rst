Pharmaceutical ERP — Equipment Qualification & Maintenance
==========================================================
GMP equipment register with IQ / OQ / PQ qualification records, calibration, cleaning and QA-verified maintenance, with fitness checks on manufacturing orders and QC results.

Configuration
=============
* Enable *Equipment Qualification & Maintenance* in Settings > Pharmaceutical ERP.
* Choose the equipment check (No Check, Warn or Block) in the same section.
* Create equipment categories with an equipment class, then register the equipment.
