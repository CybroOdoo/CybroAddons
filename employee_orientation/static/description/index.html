Answers Before You Install

Q
Does this module support Odoo 20 Community Edition?
A
Yes, it is fully certified for Odoo 20 Community Edition and also operates flawlessly on Odoo 20 Enterprise and Odoo.sh. No proprietary Enterprise license is required for core accounting operations.
 
Q
How is Post-Dated Cheque (PDC) clearing handled?
A
Cheques are held in clearing accounts until maturity. Upon clearance, the system automatically posts the bank journal entry and updates the corresponding invoice balance.
 
Q
Can I migrate historical data from Odoo 17, 18, or 19?
A
Yes. The data architecture maintains backwards compatibility, ensuring that your chart of accounts, existing journal items, and asset records transition cleanly.
