Answers Before You Install

Q
How does the franchise registration process work?
A
Customers submit a franchise request through the website registration form. Once submitted, administrators can inspect the submitted details, contact information, and proposed site location to accept or cancel the request from the backend.
 
Q
Can I send contracts to approved franchise applicants?
A
Yes. Upon approving a request, clicking "Send Contract" generates the dealership contract PDF and emails it to the applicant with a secure link to review and sign online.
 
Q
How does online digital contract signing work?
A
Applicants access their contract via the customer portal, review the full terms and target amounts, and can draw, type, or upload their signature. Once signed, the agreement is legally validated and the signed PDF is archived.
