Theme Arqora

An architectural lighting studio theme for Odoo 20. A hero room the visitor lights with the mouse, sections labelled like drawing sheets, and a refined catalogue, all built from website builder snippets on the standard Odoo eCommerce.

Dimmable Hero Room

A transparent header sits over the hero. Move the mouse across the room to light it, set the Dimmer or press Lights On.

Six Families of Light

The Collections block is filled from the shop categories, so it follows the catalogue of the website.

Objects of Light

A mosaic of signature products, taken from the products published in the shop.

Light Levels by Space

Choose a room to see the recommended lux, colour temperature and approach.

Light in Architecture

Selected projects shown as a large feature next to a list of works.

Exploded Technical Drawing

A drawing sheet with numbered parts and count-up figures that start when the block comes into view.

The Shop

The standard eCommerce of Odoo, dressed in the same design: category tabs, filters, product cards with the image swap on hover, and the Add to Cart row. The product page, cart and checkout follow it.

Ready-Made Pages

Services, Projects, Materials, Resources, About, Legal and Contact pages come with the theme, each with a full-width hero.

Contact Form

The enquiry form creates a lead in CRM and sends the visitor to a thank-you page. Architects, Interior Designers, Contractors and Customers each get their own form intro.

Key Features

Header Templates

The Arqora navbar belongs to the default header template. Any other template keeps the standard Odoo look.

Menu Overflow

Three links on each side of the logo, and every further menu in a "+" dropdown.

Builder Snippets

Every block is a snippet in the Arqora group of the website builder.

Motion

Words that light up while reading, reveals on scroll, count-up figures and a header that returns on scroll up.

Shop Ready

Equal-height product cards, swatch hover that changes the image, wishlist and filters.

CRM Leads

The contact form creates a lead and redirects to a thank-you page.
