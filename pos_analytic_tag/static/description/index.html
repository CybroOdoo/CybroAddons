Enable Analytic Accounting in User Settings

Go to Settings > Users & Companies > Users and check the Analytic Accounting box under Extra Rights for the user.

Configure Analytic Account in PoS Settings

In Point of Sale settings, scroll to POS Analytic Tags and select an Analytic Account to track costs and revenues for the shop.

Open Point of Sale Session

Launch the Point of Sale shop and set the opening cash balance in the Opening Control popup to start selling.

Add Products and Proceed to Payment

Select products from the catalog to add them to the order cart, then click the Payment button to complete the sale.

View Analytic Tag on Closed Session

Open the closed Point of Sale session to see the configured Analytic Tag displayed on the session record.

Analytic Tag Linked to PoS Orders

Every order placed during the session automatically displays and tracks the assigned Analytic Tag.

Analytic Account on Payments Tab

View the Payments tab inside the order form to see the Analytic Account linked to each payment line.

Analytic Account on Payment Form

Open the individual payment record to verify that the Analytic Account is stored with the transaction details.

Automatic Analytic Distribution on Journal Items

After closing the session, check the generated journal items to see the Analytic Distribution automatically applied to debit and credit lines.

Answers Before You Install

Q
Does this module support Odoo 20 Community and Enterprise?
A
Yes, it is fully compatible with Odoo 20 Community Edition and also operates smoothly on Odoo 20 Enterprise and Odoo.sh environments.
 
Q
How does the module apply analytic accounts to accounting lines?
A
When you close a Point of Sale session, the module automatically sets the Analytic Distribution on all accounting move lines generated by the session.
 
Q
Can I set different Analytic Accounts for different Point of Sale registers?
A
Yes, each Point of Sale configuration can have its own dedicated Analytic Account selected in settings for independent store or branch tracking.
 
Q
Are analytic accounts saved on orders and payments?
A
Yes, the analytic account is recorded and stored on every order and payment record for auditing and historical reporting.
