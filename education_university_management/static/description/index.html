Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Education University Management module in Odoo?

The Education University Management module is a comprehensive tool designed to manage the core administrative and operational functions of a university or educational institution. It supports student registration, course management, faculty assignments, exams, attendance, and more.

What are the key features of the Education University Management module?

Key features include student enrollment, course scheduling, faculty management, exam administration, grading, attendance tracking, and fee management. The module also provides reporting tools for analyzing academic and operational data.

How does the module handle student enrollment?

The module allows administrators to manage student applications, admissions, and enrollments. Student details, such as personal information, academic background, and course preferences, can be entered and tracked throughout their enrollment process.

Does the module support attendance tracking?

Yes, the module includes an attendance tracking system that allows instructors to record attendance for each session. Attendance records are maintained within the system and can be reviewed and analyzed for performance or compliance purposes.
