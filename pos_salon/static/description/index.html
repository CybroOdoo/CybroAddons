POS Salon Management

Salon and Spa POS extends Odoo Point of Sale with a complete salon workflow. Staff can book scheduled appointments and walk-ins, assign specialists and amenities, manage seats and time slots, take payment through the POS, track specialist commission, and collect customer reviews.

Key Highlights

Salon Dashboard in POS

A single screen inside Point of Sale for booking, walk-ins, running appointments, all appointments and commission.

Easy Appointment Booking

Pick services, customer, specialist, amenities, date and time slots, then confirm the booking in a few clicks.

Service Categories with Price and Duration

Services are grouped by category, with the price and time of each service shown while booking. Spa services appear when Spa Management is enabled.

Smart Time Slots

Book in 30-minute slots from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. The same seat or specialist can’t be double-booked.

Seat Management

Set the number of seats for your salon, and seats are assigned automatically. Use 0 for no limit.

Walk-in Booking

Create a booking for a customer who is already at the salon. If all seats are busy, you see the conflicts and the next free start times.

Full Appointment Control

Mark customers as arrived, complete or cancel bookings, and reschedule services that haven’t started.

Payment through POS

Pay for a booking on the normal POS payment screen. The booking becomes Paid as soon as the POS order is paid.

Specialist Commission

Enter commission manually or calculate it automatically as a percentage. Managers review it on a separate Commission screen.

Customer Reviews

When a booking is completed, the customer gets an email with a rating link. All ratings show in the backend under Reviews.

Backend Reports and Records

The Point of Sale > Salon menu has Bookings, Reviews and Commission for managers.

Simple Configuration

Turn on “Is Salon” in the POS settings and choose the options you need: spa, reviews, commission, specialists, amenities and seats.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enable the salon workflow?

Open the Point of Sale configuration and enable Is Salon, then set the options you need (specialists, amenities, seats, commission, reviews). Reopen the POS session to load the salon dashboard. A shop cannot be a Salon and a Bar/Restaurant at the same time.

What is required to create a booking?

A customer and the customer's phone number are required. Scheduled bookings also need a date and a time slot for every selected service. If the seat or specialist is already busy at that time, the system asks you to choose another time.

How are payment and commission handled?

Payment is taken on the standard POS payment screen, and the booking becomes Paid once the POS order is paid. If Apply Commission is enabled and a specialist is assigned, commission is either entered manually before payment or calculated automatically as a percentage of the booking amount, depending on the Commission Mode.

Can I cancel or reschedule a booking, and how do reviews work?

A confirmed booking can be cancelled only while it is unpaid; a paid booking needs its payment refunded first. A confirmed booking can be rescheduled until the customer is marked as arrived. If Take Review is enabled and the customer has an email address, a review link is emailed when the booking is completed.
