POS Salon Management
====================
Salon and Spa POS extends Odoo Point of Sale with a complete salon workflow.
Staff can book scheduled appointments and walk-ins, assign specialists and
amenities, manage seats and time slots, take payment through the POS, track
specialist commission, and collect customer reviews.

Configuration
=============
1. Open Point of Sale > Configuration > Point of Sale.
2. Select or create a Point of Sale configuration.
3. Enable the 'Is Salon' option. A shop cannot be both a Salon and a
   Bar/Restaurant.
4. Enable and set the salon options as required:

   * Spa Management
   * Take Review
   * Apply Commission, Commission Mode (Manually or Automatically) and
     Commission (%)
   * Show Appointments and Show All Appointments
   * Specialists and Amenities available during booking
   * Seats (0 means no limit)

5. Save the configuration and open a POS session.

When the POS session starts, the salon dashboard opens automatically for
configurations with 'Is Salon' enabled, and the standard POS product screen
is replaced by the salon screens.
