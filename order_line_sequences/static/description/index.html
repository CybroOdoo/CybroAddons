Answers Before You Install

Q
Which documents and reports will display sequence numbers?
A
Automatic sequence numbers (#) are added to order lines across Sales Quotations & Sales Orders, Purchase RFQs & Purchase Orders, and Stock Transfers (Receipts & Deliveries). In addition, sequential line numbers are printed on their respective PDF reports, including Quotations, Orders, and Picking Operations documents.
 
Q
Is any manual configuration required after installation?
A
No manual setup is required. The "Line Numbers" setting in Sales is automatically activated across all companies by default, and line sequence numbers in Purchase and Inventory operate immediately upon installation.
 
Q
How are sequence numbers handled when lines are reordered or removed?
A
Sequence numbers are dynamically computed. If you reorder lines, insert new items, or delete lines, the line numbers automatically recalculate to ensure a continuous and organized sequence (1, 2, 3...) without gaps.
