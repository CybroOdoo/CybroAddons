Oil & Gas ESG
=============
* Oil ERP ESG is a centralized solution designed to manage Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) metrics efficiently within Odoo.
* It is an open-source module developed to extend Odoo with industry-specific sustainability functionalities tailored for oil and gas businesses.
* Oil ERP ESG provides integrated features for carbon emission tracking, regulatory compliance, and sustainability reporting.
* The ESG module acts as a unified layer that standardizes all sustainability management operations within the Oil ERP system.
* It follows a specialized approach, allowing businesses to effectively manage their oil and gas environmental impact based on compliance requirements.
