Calculation Standards

Core Pillars of Hydrocarbon Correction

ASTM D1250 Groups

Performs CTL/CPL calculations for Crude Oils (Group A), Fuel Oils (Group B), Jet Fuels (Group C), Transition Zone (Group D), Gasolines (Group E), and Lubricating Oils (Group F).

GPA TP-27 LPG Tables

Employs density-specific thermal expansion CTL coefficients and Clausius-Clapeyron vapor pressure correlations (CPL) tailored for light NGL/LPG mixtures.

AGA-8 Gas virials

Computes natural gas compressibility factors (Zobs/Zstd) using Kay's mixing rules and composition inputs (CH4, C2H6, C3H8, CO2, N2, H2S).

Precision & Rounding

Applies per-standard rounding rules (e.g. 5/6 decimal places for CTL/CPL/VCF) and issues non-blocking warnings when values fall outside limits.

Configuration Settings

Hydrocarbon Product Correction Settings

Under Odoo Configuration > Settings, enabling the HPM Volume Correction checkbox activates the unified correction engine. This makes dynamic volume calculations available across product templates, categories, and inventory locations.

Product Category Configuration

Product Category HPM Setup

Product categories (such as Crude Oil) can be configured with specific standardization types, including ASTM D1250 (API MPMS 11.1), API Product Group (Group A - Crude Oils), and standard reference conditions (60°F temp, 0 psi pressure, 34 API gravity).

Product Specification View

Product HPM Inheritance

Individual products inherit standard HPM configurations from their product categories automatically, with options to override standardization type, product group, and reference temperature/pressure at the product template level.

Production Logging Wizard

Production Logging & Volume Correction Wizard

The Record Production wizard dynamically calculates the Volume Correction Factor (VCF) in real-time based on observed temperature, pressure, and API gravity inputs, outputting the standard quantity for accurate inventory logging.

Unified Volume Correction Engine

A centralized volume correction system consumed by custody transfers, production wizards, and SCADA integrations. Automatically standardizes observed volumes with high precision.

Compliance Rounding & Precision

Automatically enforces industry-mandated rounding rules for correction factor calculations (such as ASTM D1250 and local operating standards) to ensure financial and audit compliance.

LPG & Light Hydrocarbon Correction

Calculates precise correction factors (CTL/CPL) for light hydrocarbons like propane and butane mixtures across standard density ranges.

Natural Gas Compressibility

Computes compressibility factors (Z-factor) for natural gas compositions based on methane, ethane, carbon dioxide, and other gas components.

Which standards does this module support?
It supports API MPMS Chapter 11.1 / ASTM D1250 (Groups A-F), OIML R117, GPA TP-27 (LPG/NGL), AGA-8 (gas), SAES-Y-100, and AGES-SP-11-01.
How does gas compressibility (Z-factor) calculation work?
For natural gas, the engine uses Kay's mixing rules to compute the mixture critical temperature and pressure from composition mole fractions (CH4, C2H6, C3H8, CO2, N2, H2S). A simplified virial equation of state is then applied to calculate Z at observed conditions.
What happens when measurements fall outside range limits?
The validation engine automatically checks whether observed parameters (density, temperature, pressure) are within the standard's defined range. If a value falls outside, it appends human-readable warning alerts to the computation results to warn operators of potential VCF inaccuracies.
How are HPM settings managed on products?
HPM settings are configured at the product category level. Products linked to those categories automatically inherit the correction parameters. You can override inherited settings on individual product templates if needed.
