Flight Maintenance

Keeps unfit aircraft out of the schedule and tracks the metrics that drive maintenance timing — built directly on Odoo's native Maintenance app.

Key Highlights

Airworthiness Gating

While a maintenance request marked "Grounds Aircraft" is open, the aircraft is automatically kept out of scheduling and dispatch — no manual status flip required.

Built on Odoo's Maintenance App

No separate work-order model. Every aircraft is auto-linked to a maintenance.equipment record, so aircraft maintenance sits alongside any other equipment your team already tracks.

Running Aircraft Metrics

Airframe hours, engine hours, cycles, and fuel used accumulate per aircraft from individual metric entries, with live totals shown on the aircraft form.

Metrics Logged Automatically

Airframe hours and cycles are logged the moment a flight is marked Completed, calculated straight from actual departure and arrival times.

Aircraft ↔ Equipment Sync

Each aircraft's linked equipment record keeps its name in sync with the tail number and model automatically, so records never drift apart.

Dedicated Technician Access

A Flight Management / Maintenance Technician group scopes who can log metrics and manage requests, layered on top of your existing Maintenance app permissions.

Does this replace Odoo's Maintenance app?

No — it's built directly on top of it. Aircraft maintenance requests use the same stages, kanban, and workflow as any other equipment your organization already tracks in Maintenance.

What happens when a grounding request is marked Done?

Once no open grounding requests remain, the aircraft's status automatically reverts to Active — unless it has been manually set to Grounded, which is treated as permanent.

How are airframe hours and cycles recorded?

Automatically, whenever a flight is marked Completed — the duration between actual departure and arrival is logged as airframe hours, plus one cycle. Engine hours and fuel used are logged manually.

Can a maintenance request avoid grounding the aircraft?

Yes — uncheck "Grounds Aircraft" on the request. It stays attached to the aircraft and equipment for record-keeping but has no effect on scheduling.
