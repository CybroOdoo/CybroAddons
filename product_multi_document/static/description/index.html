Easily upload and manage multiple documents associated with a product.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I attach documents to a product?

You can attach documents to a product by navigating to the product form and uploading the necessary files in the document section.

Can I view the attached product documents in the Purchase Order?

Yes, all documents attached to a product will be visible in the Purchase Order lines, providing better transparency and accessibility.

Can I attach any type of file to a product?

Yes, you can attach various file types such as PDFs, images, Word documents, and more to the product, depending on your business needs.

Are the attached documents automatically sent via email when sending a Purchase Order?

Yes, the documents linked to a product will be automatically attached to emails sent with the Purchase Order, ensuring all necessary files are included.
