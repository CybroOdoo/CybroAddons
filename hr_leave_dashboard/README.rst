HR Leave Dashboard
==================

HR Leave Dashboard turns the Time Off dashboard into a team overview. Next to
your leave balances it shows your profile, your org chart, your department's
absentees and upcoming public holidays, and your team's leave approval status,
and it prints a leave report for any period.

Key Features
------------

* Employee profile card with job position, working hours, email, phone,
  department and company.
* Org chart with your manager and direct reports, their subordinate counts, and
  links to each employee and their direct, indirect or total team.
* Department card with your current shift, the team members on leave today and
  the upcoming public holidays.
* Public holidays and weekends highlighted on the yearly Time Off calendar.
* Approved, to approve and refused leave counts for each team member, filtered
  by today, this week, this month or this year.
* PDF leave report for the selected period, with allocated, taken and remaining
  days per employee and leave type.

Installation
------------
No external dependencies.
