====================================
Waste Management Contracts & Billing
====================================
Customer contracts, service level agreements (SLAs), flexible pricing rules, and consolidated billing for Waste Management in Odoo 19.

* Customer Contract management with validity dates, multi-stream lines, and automated renewal workflows.
* Flexible pricing: per-collection, per-material, per-category rate, and base fee plus overweight billing, with minimum weight and minimum charge rules.
* Ad hoc billable events (extra pickups, overweight, contamination, emergency callouts) with their own pricing rules.
* SLAs with measurable commitments, penalty terms, and breach notification emails.
* Customer Portal integration allowing clients to view services, download signed contracts, view collection orders, and submit service requests.
* Automated monthly consolidated billing runs grouping completed collection orders into single unified customer invoices.

Configuration
=============
* Create and configure Customer Contracts under ``Waste Management > Configuration > Customer > Contracts``.
* Define Pricing Rules and Rate Cards per customer or material under ``Waste Management > Billing > Pricing Rules``.
* Configure SLAs and alert escalation triggers under ``Waste Management > Configuration > Customer > SLAs``.
* Schedule automated monthly billing runs under ``Waste Management > Billing > Invoicing & Runs > Monthly Billing Runs``.
