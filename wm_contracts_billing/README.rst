====================================
Waste Management Contracts & Billing
====================================
Customer contracts, service level agreements (SLAs), flexible pricing rules, and consolidated billing for Waste Management in Odoo 19.

* Customer Contract management with validity dates, multi-stream lines, and automated renewal workflows.
* Multi-tier and tiered pricing rules: Flat Rate, Per-KG, Minimum Charge, Excess Weight/Volume charges, and Container Rental fees.
* SLA tracking with response time thresholds, priority levels, and breach warning notifications.
* Customer Portal integration allowing clients to view services, download signed contracts, and track manifests.
* Automated monthly consolidated billing runs grouping completed collection orders into single unified customer invoices.

Configuration
=============
* Create and configure Customer Contracts under ``Waste Management > Billing > Contracts``.
* Define Pricing Rules and Rate Cards per customer or material under ``Waste Management > Billing > Pricing Rules``.
* Configure SLAs and alert escalation triggers under ``Waste Management > Billing > SLAs``.
* Schedule automated monthly billing runs under ``Waste Management > Billing > Monthly Billing Runs``.
