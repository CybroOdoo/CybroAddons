How does the monthly consolidated billing run work?
When you execute a monthly billing run, the engine queries all completed or signed, unbilled collection orders of customers whose Billing Mode is Monthly Billing Run, prices each collection from its contract, rate card or pricing rule, and generates draft customer invoices (one per contract) itemized by waste category. Confirmed billable events are added to the same invoice. The monthly cron also runs this automatically on the 1st.
Can we set different pricing tiers for different waste materials?
Yes. Contracts support 4 billing bases (per collection, per material, hybrid, and category rate). Per-kg pricing rules, set per waste category and optionally per customer, add minimum weight and minimum charge, and per-collection and hybrid contracts add configurable overweight pricing per 10 kg.
Are ad-hoc service charges billed automatically?
Ad-hoc billable events (extra pickups, overweight, contamination, emergency callouts) are priced from partner or global event pricing rules and, once confirmed, are added to the customer's next monthly billing run or consolidated invoice. Container rental and lease charges are not handled by this module.
Does the module integrate with standard Odoo Invoicing and Accounting?
Yes. All generated customer invoices are native Odoo account.move records with standard journal entries, payment matching, and financial reporting compatibility.
