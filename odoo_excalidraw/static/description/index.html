Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find the Sketch app?

On the Odoo main App Dashboard/Home screen. Look for the Sketch app icon (the Excalidraw logo). Click it to open the full whiteboard canvas.

Why can't I see some models in the Attach wizard?

The model list is filtered to only show: Models that have an active chatter/mail thread (mail.thread) — so that the attachment can be seen in the record's chatter. Models that the current logged-in user has write access to — for security and data integrity. Internal Odoo technical models (like ir.*, mail.* logs, bus.*, utm.*, etc.) are excluded from the list.

What formats can I download directly to my local system or attach to business records in Odoo?

Image (PNG), PDF Document, Excel Spreadsheet, Word Document
