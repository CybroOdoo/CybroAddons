How does waste batch intake integrate with Collection routes?
When a collection route (or a standalone collection order) is completed, draft waste batches are created per waste category from the collected weights. Once received into stock, they can be sorted or recycled.
How are EPA WARM carbon avoidance numbers calculated?
Each waste category has a configurable EPA WARM emission avoidance factor (warm_factor_co2, defaulting to 1.50 tCO2e/tonne). The system multiplies the recovered weight (converted to kg, divided by 1,000) of each recovery line by this factor to determine the avoided greenhouse gas emissions.
Does the module support inventory stock moves and valuation?
Yes. When a recycling order is marked as done, it creates native Odoo stock moves that consume the waste from the batch location and increase on-hand quantities of the recovered products at their destination locations. Recovered products are valued at their own product cost; no cost is allocated from the waste. Unrecovered residue is moved to a Recycling Process Loss location.
Can we sell recovered secondary materials directly from recycling orders?
Yes. On a done recycling order, one click opens a new sales order pre-populated with the recovered products, quantities and the sale prices set on the recovery lines (taken from the product sales price by default); you choose the customer and save it.
