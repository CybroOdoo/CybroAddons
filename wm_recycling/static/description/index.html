Frequent questions

How are carbon avoided metrics calculated?
The module incorporates the EPA Waste Reduction Model (WARM) carbon factors. When a recycling order is completed, the system multiplies the actual recovered quantity of each material by its verified EPA WARM emission factor to compute metric tons of CO2 equivalent (MTCO2e) avoided.
Does recovered material integrate directly with Odoo Inventory?
Yes - upon completing a Recycling Order, the system automatically creates stock moves depositing the recovered commodities into your designated raw materials or finished goods warehouse location.
Can we sell recovered secondary commodities directly to customers?
Yes, the module integrates with Odoo Sales, allowing you to generate sales quotations, delivery orders, and customer invoices directly from recovered commodity stock.
Can recovery rates be tracked across multiple processing facilities?
Yes, the Recycling Efficiency Pivot Report allows you to compare conversion yields, scrap loss, and throughput rates across different facilities, teams, and processing lines.
Do facility operators need to manually enter raw waste batches before recycling?
No. Raw waste batches are automatically created and weighed when collection routes finish in the collection module. Facility operators simply select the incoming batch, launch the Guided Sorting Wizard to separate recyclable fractions, and proceed directly to the Recycling Work Order.
