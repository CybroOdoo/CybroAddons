Odoo Pipedrive Connector
========================

This module helps to import products, contacts and leads from Pipedrive to
Odoo, and to export them from Odoo to Pipedrive. All import and export
operations can be performed with a single button click.

Key Features
------------

* Import products, product categories, contacts and leads from Pipedrive to Odoo
* Export products, contacts and leads from Odoo to Pipedrive
* Sync products, contacts and leads with a single button click from the company form
* Products, contacts and leads created, updated or deleted in Odoo are synced to Pipedrive
* Products and contacts added, updated or deleted in Pipedrive are synced to Odoo through webhooks

Installation
------------
No external dependencies.

Configuration
-------------
Please note that this feature is only supported on secure (HTTPS) URLs. Also use a DB filter.

* First you need to create a Pipedrive token.

1) Go to https://demo-sandbox4.pipedrive.com/settings/api and log in to your account.
2) Click on your profile picture at the right corner.
3) Click on Personal preferences.
4) Under API, you can see the Pipedrive token.
