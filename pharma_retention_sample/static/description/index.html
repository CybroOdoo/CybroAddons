Feature Activation

Activate Retention Samples from Settings

The register is switched on in the Pharmaceutical ERP configuration, where the default retention storage location is also chosen.

Product Setup

Retention Rules on the Product

Products say whether a reserve sample is required, how long it is held beyond expiry, its default container and storage location, and how often it is visually examined.

From the Batch

Retention Sample Button on the Batch and QA Release

A released batch offers a Retention Sample button on the lot and on its QA release, creating the draft record with the batch, product, container and storage location filled in; the quantity set aside is entered before confirming.

Register

Retention Sample Register

Samples move from Draft to Retained to Disposed. Confirming requires the storage location and a quantity, and sets the next examination date.

Locations

Retention Storage Locations

Stock locations are marked as retention storage so reserve samples are held in a controlled, identifiable place.

Withdrawal

Record a Withdrawal with Its Purpose

Withdrawals log the date, quantity and purpose. A periodic examination also records a satisfactory or unsatisfactory result and rolls the schedule forward.

Examination

Examination Due List

A worklist shows samples due for periodic examination, and an unsatisfactory result suggests raising a deviation for the batch.

Disposal

Approved Disposal Wizard

Quality management disposes of a sample through a wizard that records the method: incineration, chemical destruction or a licensed waste contractor.

Reminders

Missing Reserve Samples

A daily check reminds the releasing user of released batches whose product requires a reserve sample but has none confirmed. Release is never held up; the gap is made visible instead.

Retention Register

Draft, Retained and Disposed samples linked to the batch and storage location.

Withdrawals & Exams

Purpose-coded withdrawals and scheduled periodic examination.

Disposal Control

QA-only disposal with a recorded method.

Worklists

Missing Reserve Samples, Examination Due and Due for Disposal.
Does a missing reserve sample block batch release?
No. Release is not held up. A daily reminder makes the gap visible to the releasing user.
Who can dispose of a retention sample?
Only quality management, through the disposal wizard, which records the disposal method.
What happens after a periodic examination?
The last and next examination dates roll forward by the sample's interval. An unsatisfactory result adds a note recommending a deviation for the batch.
