Configure Sales Automation Settings

Navigate to Sales → Configuration → Settings and scroll down to the "Automate Orders" section to view and manage sales automation options.

Enable Confirm Quotation

Enable the "Confirm Quotation" checkbox in Sales Settings to automatically convert newly created quotations into confirmed sales orders.

Auto-Confirmed Sales Order

Create a quotation and save. A confirmed quotation is automatically created as a "Sales Order" without requiring manual confirmation.

Enable "Confirm Quotation" and Create Invoice

Enable both "Confirm Quotation" and "Create Invoice" checkboxes to automate both order confirmation and invoice generation simultaneously.

Automatic Draft Invoice Generation

Saving the quotation automatically confirms the sales order and generates a draft customer invoice, visible in the "Invoices" smart button.

Enable "Confirm Quotation", "Create Invoice" & Validate Invoice

Enable "Validate Invoice" alongside "Confirm Quotation" and "Create Invoice" to take automation to the next stage by automatically posting the invoice.

Auto-Validated Customer Invoice

Upon saving the quotation, the order is confirmed, an invoice is generated, and it is automatically validated and posted in "Posted" status.

Enable Print Invoices Option

Check all four automation options in Sales Settings: "Confirm Quotation", "Create Invoice", "Validate Invoice", and "Print Invoices".

Direct Print Invoice Button on Sales Order

With "Print Invoices" enabled, a dedicated "Print Invoice" action button appears directly on the Sales Order form header to quickly print the customer invoice.

Configure Purchase Automation Settings

Navigate to Purchase → Configuration → Settings and locate the "Automate Orders" section to configure purchase automation.

Enable Confirm RFQ

Check the "Confirm RFQ" setting in Purchase Settings to automate Request for Quotation conversion into confirmed purchase orders upon saving.

Auto-Confirmed Purchase Order

Create a Request for Quotation (RFQ) and save it. The RFQ is automatically confirmed and transitioned directly into "Purchase Order" status.

Enable "Confirm RFQ" and Print Bills

Enable both "Confirm RFQ" and "Print Bills" options under the Purchase Automate Orders configuration to unlock bill printing functionality.

Direct Print Bill Button on Purchase Order

Upon saving the RFQ, a confirmed purchase order is created and a dedicated "Print Bill" button appears on the order header to quickly print vendor bills.

Answers Before You Install

Q
What core operations does this module automate?
A
The module automatically converts new Quotations into confirmed Sales Orders and Requests for Quotation (RFQs) into Purchase Orders upon saving. For sales, it can also automatically generate Customer Invoices and immediately validate (post) them upon confirmation. In addition, it provides direct one-click Print Invoice and Print Bill buttons right from order form headers.
 
Q
Can I enable quotation confirmation without automatically generating invoices?
A
Yes, all automation options are modular and independently configurable. Under Sales > Settings and Purchase > Settings, you can toggle Automate Sale, Automate Invoice, Automate Validate Invoice, and Print Invoices / Bills separately according to your company requirements.
 
Q
Does this module support Odoo 20 Community and Enterprise Editions?
A
Yes, it is fully certified for Odoo 20 Community Edition and also operates flawlessly on Odoo 20 Enterprise Edition and Odoo.sh. It extends standard Odoo Sales, Purchase, and Invoicing models and requires no third-party libraries.
