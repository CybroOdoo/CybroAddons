Automate Sale And Purchase Orders
=================================
Automate the sale and purchase orders flow.

Key Features
------------
* **Automate Quotation Confirmation**: Automatically converts new quotations into confirmed Sales Orders upon saving.
* **Automate Customer Invoice Creation**: Automatically generates customer draft invoices upon sales order confirmation.
* **Automate Invoice Validation**: Automatically posts and validates customer invoices immediately upon creation.
* **Invoicing Policy Verification**: Ensures order line products use the ordered invoicing policy before generating customer invoices.
* **Direct Print Invoice Button**: Adds a convenient "Print Invoice" action button directly on the Sales Order form header for quick PDF printing.
* **Automate RFQ Confirmation**: Automatically converts Requests for Quotation (RFQs) into confirmed Purchase Orders upon creation.
* **Direct Print Bill Button**: Adds a dedicated "Print Bill" action button directly on the Purchase Order form header for quick vendor bill printing.
* **Website Order Safeguard**: Excludes eCommerce/website orders from auto-confirmation to preserve online checkout and payment transactions.
* **Modular Configuration**: Independent toggle switches under Sales and Purchase settings to customize your automation workflow.
