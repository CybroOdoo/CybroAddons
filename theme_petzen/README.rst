Theme Petzen
============
A modern and responsive website theme designed for pet shops and pet care services.

Overview
========
Theme Petzen provides a complete website and eCommerce experience tailored for
pet-related businesses: home, shop, cart, services, about, reviews and contact
pages, dynamic "Shop by Category" and "Top Picks" snippets, quick view, service
booking and customer reviews.

Configuration
=============
* Install the theme from Website > Configuration > Settings > Choose a Theme
  (or the theme selection of a new website).
* Open a product category and enable **Show in Petzen Snippets** to show it in
  the "Shop by Category" block. Products assigned to that category or one of
  its sub-categories are shown in the "Top Picks" block.
* Approve the customer reviews from Website > Configuration > Customer Reviews.
* Service booking requests sent from the /services page are created as CRM
  leads ("[Petzen] Booking: <service>").
* Demo data adds a sample "Petzen" product category tree (Dog, Cat, Bird, Fish,
  Accessories) for internal and eCommerce categories.
