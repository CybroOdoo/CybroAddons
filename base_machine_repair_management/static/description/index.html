Machine Repair Management for Odoo 20

Machine Repair Management is an application used to manage machine repairs

Email Us Whatsapp
Website Repair Requests Customer Portal Tracking Repair Teams Diagnosis & Quotation Work Orders Repair Images Customer Feedback

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Create Requests from the Website

Customers submit machine repair requests directly from your website. They are created in the backend automatically.

 

Track Status from Portal

Customers see the list and details of their repair requests, with status, on their portal.

 

Diagnosis and Quotation

Create a diagnosis from a repair, add the parts needed, and generate a sales quotation for the customer.

 

Repair Teams and Work Orders

Assign repairs to teams, then create work orders with schedule, duration and hours spent.

 

Repair Images

Attach up to five images to each repair request.

 

Repair Summary PDF Report

Print a PDF summary of all repairs between two dates.

 

Close Repairs and Notify Customers

Close or reopen a repair, and email the customer when it is closed.

 

Customer Feedback

Customers rate the service (Poor, Average, Good, Excellent) and leave comments through the website.

Answers Before You Install

Q
What is the Base Machine Repair Management module?
A
The module helps businesses manage machine repairs by tracking requests, assigning technicians, monitoring repair progress, and maintaining repair history.
 
Q
Can I track machine repair requests?
A
Yes, the module allows users to log repair requests, assign them to technicians, and monitor their status.
 
Q
Can I assign repairs to teams and create work orders?
A
Yes. Create teams with a lead and members, assign the repair using Assign to Team, then click Create Work Order to plan the job.
