Inter Company Stock Transfer
============================
Inter Company Stock Transfer

- Create counterpart Receipt/Delivery Orders between companies

Configuration
=============
1. Go to **Settings → Users & Companies → Companies**.
2. Select a company and navigate to the **Inter Company Transfer** tab.
3. Enable the **Inter Company Transfer** boolean.
4. Choose the operation type in **Apply On** (*Delivery and Receipts*, *Receipt*, or *Delivery Order*).
5. Select the destination **Warehouse** for the company.
6. Configure the participating companies with their corresponding transfer options.
