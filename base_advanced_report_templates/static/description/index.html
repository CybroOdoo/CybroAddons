Frequently Asked Questions

What is the purpose of the Odoo Professional Report Templates module?

The module allows users to customize the design and appearance of PDF reports such as Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, Invoices, and Delivery Orders using predefined templates and color configurations.

Which document types are supported by this module?

The module supports Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, Customer Invoices, Vendor Bills, and Delivery Orders/Stock Pickings.

Can I customize the colors used in reports?

Yes. Users can configure colors for table headers, titles, general text, customer addresses, and company addresses to match their company branding.
