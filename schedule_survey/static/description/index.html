Enable Survey Scheduling (Cron)

After installing the module, open a Survey and enable the “Enable Cron” option to activate survey scheduling.

Set Scheduled Date

Once Cron is enabled, select a Scheduled Date on which the survey should be automatically sent to participants.

Configure Sharing Options

Using the Sharing Option, select one or more participants to whom the survey should be sent.

Automatic Survey Email Dispatch

On the scheduled date, the system automatically sends the survey email to all selected participants without manual action.

Cron Status Update

After the survey email is sent successfully, the Cron Status is updated to Done, confirming successful execution.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I schedule a survey for a future date?

Yes. You can set a specific scheduled date for the survey after enabling the Cron option.

Key Features:
  • Date-based survey scheduling
  • Cron automation support
  • Planned survey campaigns

Can I send the survey to multiple participants?

Yes. Using the Sharing Option, you can select multiple participants to receive the survey.

Key Features:
  • Multiple recipient selection
  • Flexible sharing configuration
  • Mass survey distribution

How do I know if the survey was sent successfully?

After successful email delivery, the Cron Status automatically changes to Done.

Key Features:
  • Cron status tracking
  • Execution confirmation
  • Reliable automated delivery
