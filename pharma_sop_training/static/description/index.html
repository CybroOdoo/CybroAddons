Why Pharmaceutical ERP?

Settings

Enable SOP

Turn on SOP & Training Management from Pharmaceutical ERP > Configuration > Settings. This unlocks the SOP and training menus and activates training-gated sign-offs on manufacturing steps.

SOP

SOP & Trainings

Once enabled, dedicated menus for Standard Operating Procedures, Training Records, and My Trainings appear in the app, giving QA and HR a single place to manage documents and employee compliance.

SOP

Create SOP

Draft a new Standard Operating Procedure with its title, department, version, and instructions. Every SOP starts in the Draft state before moving through review.

SOP

Approve SOP

Move the SOP from Draft through Review to Effective. Once approved, the document becomes the active reference version that operations and training records point to.

Training

Assess Training Records

Review each employee's training against the linked SOP, mark it Passed or Failed, and set a validity period. Failed or expired training blocks the employee from being assigned as a qualified operator.

BOM

Link SOP With BOM Operations

Attach the relevant SOP directly to a routing operation on the Bill of Materials, so operators are shown the correct procedure the moment they start that step.

MO

Create MO

Once you create a Manufacturing Order and confirm it, the BMR is automatically generated, carrying forward whichever SOPs were linked to each routing operation.

BMR

BMR

The Batch Manufacturing Record lists each operation together with its linked SOP, so operators can consult the correct procedure and supervisors can confirm only currently trained staff signed off each step.

Document Control

Version control and state management for all Standard Operating Procedures.

Training Matrix

Assign and track training requirements for different roles and procedures.

Automated Expiry

Daily cron jobs automatically check and flag expired training certifications.

Execution Gates

Prevent untrained operators from signing off on critical BMR steps.
What is the purpose of this module?
It ensures that all personnel are adequately trained on current SOPs before they can execute critical manufacturing tasks.
How are training expirations handled?
A daily automated cron job checks training records and updates statuses, ensuring operators with expired training cannot proceed.
Are SOPs linked to production?
Yes, SOPs can be linked to routing operations and BOM approvals, serving as execution gates for BMR steps.
