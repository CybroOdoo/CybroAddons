The Liquid Glass Backend Theme for Odoo 17 brings a futuristic, ultra-modern frosted glass aesthetic to your entire Odoo workspace. Crafted with precision glassmorphism, translucent dark surfaces, and luminous teal accents, it transforms daily enterprise operations into an inspiring visual experience. This theme elevates productivity with enhanced visual hierarchy, crystal-clear readability, and ergonomic eye comfort for prolonged use. It seamlessly overhauls Kanban, List, Form, Activity, and Chatter views while introducing a collapsible glass App Drawer navigation that automatically showcases your company branding.

Please make sure that you install all your required apps prior to installing or upgrading this theme for the best onboarding experience.

Overview

The Liquid Glass Backend Theme V17 is a cutting-edge visual overhaul for Odoo 17.0. Engineered with translucent layers, subtle specular borders, and high-performance backdrop blurs, it strikes the perfect balance between futuristic elegance and daily business efficiency. Every interface component—from floating rich-text toolbars and stage progress bars to date pickers and smart buttons—has been polished for an immaculate enterprise workflow with zero frontend leaks.

  • Translucent Glassmorphism & Frosted Glass Surfaces
  • Luminous Teal & Cyan Accents with High Readability Contrast
  • Complete Overhaul of All Backend Apps & Views
  • Polished Glass Modals, Wizards & Floating Rich-Text Toolbars
  • Ergonomic Eye Comfort for Long Working Sessions
  • Collapsible Glass Sidebar Drawer with Auto Company Logo
New

All-New Menu Design

The redesigned navigation drawer features translucent glass styling with smooth collapsible side navigation. Applications are represented with minimalist, crystal-clear icons and luminous hover states. The sidebar automatically retrieves and presents your active Company Logo, giving your ERP an instant branded feel with effortless toggle controls for full-screen focus.
