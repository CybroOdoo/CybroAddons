Advanced Accounting Dashboard Pro
==================================

* Enterprise-grade, role-aware accounting dashboard with dynamic KPIs, charts, and smart alerts for Odoo 20.0

Key Features
------------
* **17+ Real-Time KPI Cards & Financial Ratios**: Track Revenue, Expenses, Net Profit, Cash Balance, Receivables, Payables, Gross Margin, DSO, DPO, and Runway Days.
* **10 Interactive Financial Charts**: Revenue vs Expense, Cash Flow Forecast, Aging Analysis, 12-Month P&L Trend, Cash Inflow/Outflow, Top Expenses, Expense Breakdown, Income vs Expense, Budget vs Actual, and Cashflow Waterfall.
* **Operational Action Lists**: Instant access to Overdue Customer Invoices, Upcoming Vendor Bills, and Recent Payments with direct record links.
* **Smart Alerts & Quick Launchpad**: Real-time operational warnings for overdue receivables, pending payables, and unreconciled entries with 1-click shortcuts.
* **Dynamic Period Filtering**: Instant filtering by predefined periods (This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, Last Quarter, This Year, Last Year) or custom date ranges.
* **Owl Framework & Dark Mode**: High-performance reactive architecture built for Odoo 20 with seamless 1-click Light and Dark mode switching.
