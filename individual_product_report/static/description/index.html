Answers Before You Install

Q
What does the Product Sale Report Graph View module do?
A
This module introduces a dedicated Sales Report smart button to product template and product variant form views. Clicking the button opens an interactive graph report (Line, Bar, or Pie chart) displaying confirmed sales quantities specifically filtered for that product.
 
Q
Does this report consider cancelled or draft sales orders?
A
No, the report exclusively reflects confirmed and done sales orders in 'sale' and 'done' states, guaranteeing accurate and dependable commercial sales analytics.
 
Q
When is the Sales Report smart button visible?
A
The smart button automatically appears on saleable products (where "Can be Sold" is enabled) that have at least one confirmed sale (sales count greater than zero), keeping the interface clean for unsold or non-saleable items.
 
Q
Can I analyze sales for an individual variant as well as the whole product template?
A
Yes. Clicking the button from a product template presents consolidated sales for all associated variants, while clicking it from a specific variant record isolates data exclusively for that variant.
 
Q
Can I switch between chart types and customize data grouping?
A
Yes. You can switch among Line, Bar, and Pie chart views with a single click. Furthermore, you can use Odoo's native search bar to apply custom date filters and group data by customer, salesperson, or sales team.
 
Q
Who can access and view the sales report graphs?
A
Access is strictly protected by Odoo security groups. Users with Salesman (User: Own Documents Only or User: All Documents) and Sales Administrator permissions can access the smart button and view graphs. Unauthorized users are prevented from executing the action.
