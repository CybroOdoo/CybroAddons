Announcement Type: By Employee

Announcement type is selected as Employee. Select an employee and click Send for Approval.

Announcement Type: By Department

When the announcement type is set to By Department, you can select one or more departments as the target audience. Only employees belonging to those departments will see the announcement once it is approved. Fill in the details and click Send for Approval.

Announcement Type: By Job Position

When the announcement type is set to By Job Position, you can select specific job positions as the target audience. Only employees holding those job positions will see the announcement once approved. Fill in the details and click Send for Approval.

What types of announcements can be created in the app?

General Announcements: Visible to all employees.
Employee Announcements: Targeted to specific employees.
Departmental Announcements: Targeted to employees within specific departments.
Job Position Announcements: Directed to employees with specific job positions.

Where can I view the announcements?

From the corresponding employee form view.

Can I view announcements submitted for other employees?

Announcements created in draft can be approved or rejected by an authorized person (those who have administrative access).
