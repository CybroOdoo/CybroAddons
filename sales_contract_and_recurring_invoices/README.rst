Sales Contract and Recurring Invoices
=====================================

The Sales Contract and Recurring Invoices module facilitates the creation and management of customer subscription contracts in Odoo 20. It automates recurring invoice generation based on customizable billing intervals, tracks contract expiration lifecycles, and provides a dedicated customer portal interface where clients can view contract details and download PDF subscription reports.

Key Features
------------

* Create and manage subscription contracts with flexible billing intervals and recurring invoicing.
* Automated draft invoice creation via daily scheduled action when contract reaches the next invoice date.
* Invoice smart button on contract form to quickly track and inspect all related invoices.
* Lock and unlock contracts to prevent unwanted edits while allowing modifications when needed.
* Automatic contract stage progression through Ongoing, Expiring Soon, Expired, and Cancelled.
* Dedicated Sale Order Lines tab linking products and orders associated with the subscription contract.
* Customer portal self-service under My Account > My Contracts to track active contracts.
* Print and download professional subscription contract PDF reports directly from backend and portal views.
