Oil & Gas Joint Venture
=======================
* Oil ERP Joint Venture is a centralized solution designed to manage Joint Operating Agreements, partner interests, and cost-and-revenue accounting efficiently within Odoo.
* It is an open-source module developed to extend Odoo with industry-specific joint venture functionalities tailored for oil and gas businesses.
* Oil ERP Joint Venture provides integrated features for managing JOAs, Authority for Expenditure approvals, monthly Cash Calls, Joint Interest Billing, and Revenue Distribution.
* The Joint Venture module acts as a unified layer that standardizes all partner accounting operations within the Oil ERP system.
* It follows a specialized approach, allowing businesses to effectively manage their oil and gas joint ventures with Working Interest and Net Revenue Interest splits based on operational requirements.
