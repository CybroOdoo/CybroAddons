Pet Shop Management
===================

This module is used to manage pet sales and sitting services. It allows users to schedule pet sittings, which can be
viewed and managed from the website portal.

Key Features
------------
* **Pet Life Cycle & Profile Management**: Auto-generated identification sequence (PS/PT/XXX), breed and species taxonomy, dynamic age calculation, photo avatars, and real-time inventory linkage.
* **Veterinary Health & Vaccination Tracking**: Structured health questionnaires for medicine/vaccine allergies, past surgical history, vaccination dose logging, expiration tracking, and certified attending veterinarians.
* **Pet Sitting & Walker Dispatch Engine**: Interactive popup wizard to book sitting appointments directly from sales quotations, assign certified sitters/walkers, set appointment start and end times, and record attendees.
* **Employee Shifts & Staff Roles**: Dedicated employee classification flags for clinic veterinarians and dog walkers/sitters, alongside working time shift configurations.
* **Visual Interactive Calendar**: Real-time calendar view displaying all confirmed pet sitting appointments, assigned staff, and scheduled service timeframes for clear visibility.
* **One-Click Sales Quotations**: Instant sales order and quotation generation directly from pet profiles for pet purchases and sitting services.
* **24/7 Customer Self-Service Portal**: Responsive portal interface (``/my/pets`` and ``/my/sittings``) allowing pet owners to review pet profiles, check vaccine expiry dates, and track sitting schedules online.
* **Printable PDF Pet Information Reports**: Standardized, audit-ready Pet Information Report PDF documents generated from the backend action menu or downloaded directly by pet owners from the portal.
* **Multi-Tier Role-Based Security**: Granular access control with Pet User, Pet Manager, and Pet Sales Manager security groups.
