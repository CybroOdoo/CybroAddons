Answers Before You Install

Q
Does this module support Odoo 20 Community and Enterprise?
A
Yes, it is fully certified for Odoo 20 Community Edition and also operates flawlessly on Odoo 20 Enterprise and Odoo.sh. No proprietary third-party libraries or enterprise-only licenses are required.
 
Q
Can pet owners track sitting appointments and medical history online?
A
Yes! The module provides a modern, responsive customer portal (/my/pets and /my/sittings). Pet owners can review their animals' health profiles, check vaccination expiry dates, view scheduled sitting dates, and download reports.
 
Q
How are pet sitting appointments and walker schedules managed?
A
Pet sitting schedules are booked directly from sales quotations using the interactive "Create Pet Sitting Schedule" wizard. The system assigns designated walkers/sitters, sets appointment timeframes, logs attendees, and synchronizes appointments with the visual calendar.
