Frequently Asked Questions

The Google Gemini API key is required for the AI to analyze payment histories and generate risk scores. You can obtain one by creating a project in the Google AI Studio. Once you have the key, enter it in the Invoicing > Configuration > Settings menu under "Predict Late Payments AI Configuration".

Risk scores can be updated manually by clicking the "Refresh" button on the partner's form or from the AI Payment Risk Dashboard. You can also configure a scheduled action (cron job) to automatically recalculate the scores periodically (e.g., daily or weekly).

Yes! The module fully supports multi-company environments. Risk scores are calculated independently per company, meaning a single customer can have different risk scores in different companies based on their payment behavior in each specific company.

If the Gemini AI key is missing or invalid, the module will gracefully fall back to a built-in statistical scoring model. It will still analyze the customer's payment history, calculate delays, and assign a basic risk level, ensuring you still get actionable insights even without AI.
