Configure Company Data Visibility

Navigate to Website → Configuration → Websites, select your website record, and switch to the Contact Us tab. Under the Company Data Visibility section, toggle on the company details you want to display on the website, such as Company Name, Address, Phone, Email, Website, and VAT.

Company Details on Contact Us Page

Open the website's Contact Us page. The selected company information—including the company name, multi-line address with location pin icon, direct phone number, email address, and website link—is dynamically displayed alongside the contact inquiry form.

Display Options for Address & Marker Icons

In the Contact Us tab under the Options group, enable Address in one line to format the company address compactly on a single line, and activate Hide Marker Icons to remove address, phone, mail, and web icons from the website view.

Single-Line Address & Icon-Free Display

On the frontend Contact Us page, the complete address is cleanly presented in a single continuous line, and marker icons are hidden for an uncluttered and streamlined presentation.

Configure Company Social Media Links

Under the Social Links section of the Contact Us tab, toggle on the social platforms you want to display—such as Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram, and GitHub—and enter your organization's custom profile handles or URLs.

Social Media Links on Contact Us Page

The active social channels appear right on the Contact Us page with brand icons and clickable hyperlinks, allowing website visitors to directly access and connect with your social media profiles.

Answers Before You Install

Q
Where do I configure which company details appear on the Contact Us page?
A
Navigate to Website → Configuration → Websites, select your active website record, and switch to the Contact Us tab. From there, you can toggle on or off each company detail (name, address, phone, email, website, VAT) as well as display options and social links.
 
Q
How do I display the full address in a single line or hide marker icons?
A
Under the Options section in the Contact Us tab, enable the Address in one line toggle to display the address on a single line, and activate Hide Marker Icons to remove marker icons for a clean, minimalist style.
 
Q
Which social media platforms can be displayed on the Contact Us page?
A
The module supports Facebook, Twitter / X, LinkedIn, Instagram, and GitHub. Simply enable the desired toggles under the Social Links section and enter your profile links.
