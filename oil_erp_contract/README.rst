Oil & Gas Contract
==================
* Oil ERP Contract is a centralized solution designed to manage vendor and contractor agreements, lifecycle states, and billing operations efficiently within Odoo.
* It is an open-source module developed to extend Odoo with industry-specific contract management functionalities tailored for oil and gas businesses.
* Oil ERP Contract provides integrated features for managing vendor contracts, lease-linked agreements, and one-click vendor bill generation.
* The Contract module acts as a unified layer that standardizes all commercial agreement operations within the Oil ERP system.
* It follows a specialized approach, allowing businesses to effectively manage their oil and gas contracts across projects, reservoirs, and equipment based on operational requirements.
