# NHS Trust Management — Reports & Documents

Board-ready NHS Trust outputs in one click — branded PDF profiles, filtered
Excel directories and built-in document management for every Trust record in
your Odoo deployment.

## Why this module

Trust corporate teams spend hours each month assembling Trust briefings, board
papers and external reporting packs from disparate spreadsheets and slide
decks. Reports & Documents turns that work into a single button press —
producing professionally branded outputs that look like they came from the
Trust's own corporate communications team.

## Key features

**NHS-branded Trust Profile PDF** — single-click PDF for any Trust covering
  identification, governance, financials, sites, CQC inspection history,
  workforce and capacity. Header in NHS Blue (#005EB8), structured sections,
  professional typography, generated timestamp footer.
**Excel Trust Directory export** — filter by health system, region or
  status, then generate a 23-column Excel directory with auto-filter, frozen
  panes and NHS-blue header row. Ready for analyst hand-off or board reports.
**Document attachment management** — attach CQC reports, annual accounts,
  board minutes and policy documents directly to Trust and inspection records
  via Odoo's native attachment system.
**One-click print binding** — "Print Trust Profile" button appears directly
  on every Trust form once installed. No menu hunting.
**Filtered exports** — narrow your directory export to just Active trusts,
  just England, just a specific region or any combination.

## Sample outputs

The Trust Profile PDF includes:

Header band with Trust name, ODS code and FT flag
Identification (legal name, short name, type, status, dates)
Organisational hierarchy (Region, ICB, ICS or Health Board)
Contact details
Governance section with Chair, CEO and full board member table
Financials with budget, income, expenditure, colour-coded surplus/deficit,
  capital and PFI
Workforce and capacity summary
Site list with type, location, beds and A&E flag
Complete CQC inspection history with all six ratings

The Excel Directory export includes:

23 columns covering identification, hierarchy, governance, CQC, financials,
  contact and infrastructure
Auto-filter on every column
Frozen top row and first two columns for analyst use
NHS-blue header styling
