Frequently Asked Questions

What invoice information can I view directly from a sales order?

Users can view the invoiced amount, paid amount, and outstanding (due) amount directly from the sales order, eliminating the need to navigate to individual invoices.

Can I track the invoiced, paid, and outstanding amounts without opening the customer invoice?

Yes. The module displays invoice payment details directly in the sales order list and form views, providing quick visibility into payment status..

Does this module allow users to update or adjust the amount to be paid from the sales order screen?

Yes. Authorized users can modify the amount to be paid directly from the sales order, simplifying payment management and follow-up activities.
