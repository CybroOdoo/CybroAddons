Answers Before You Install

Q
How are commissions calculated in the CRM Kit?
A
Commissions can be calculated using two primary methods: Product-wise (applying category or product-specific percentages with optional maximum payout caps) and Revenue-wise (either a flat percentage across total revenue or tiered graduated brackets based on total sales thresholds).
 
Q
How do I assign a commission plan to a salesperson or sales team?
A
You can assign a plan directly on the salesperson's user profile or on the CRM Sales Team configuration form. When generating reports, the system automatically prioritizes the individual salesperson's plan; if none is assigned, it falls back seamlessly to the sales team's default plan.
 
Q
Can I export commission statements to Microsoft Excel?
A
Yes. The Commission Report wizard under CRM > Reporting > Commission Report allows you to generate and download comprehensive XLSX spreadsheets containing team summaries, salesperson totals, and commission amounts.
