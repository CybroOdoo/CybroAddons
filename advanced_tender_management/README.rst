Advanced Tender Management
==========================

Run your whole tender process in Odoo: publish tenders on your website, let
registered vendors submit their bids online, evaluate and rank the bids, and
turn the best offer into a purchase order.

Key Features
------------

* Tender stages from draft to bid submission, evaluation, selection and closing;
  a daily scheduled action opens bidding on the bid start date and closes it
  after the bid end date.
* Vendors register on the website and get portal access automatically or after
  approval.
* Vendors download the tender documents, enter their prices and attach their own
  documents on the tender page; bids received elsewhere are entered and
  submitted in the backend.
* Vendors ask to edit a submitted bid while bidding is open; the tender
  responsible approves it.
* Bids are qualified or disqualified during evaluation, and qualified bids are
  ranked by their total.
* Award the whole tender to one bid, or choose the best vendor for each product;
  confirming creates the purchase orders.
* Import and export the tender product lines as an XLSX file; quantities accept
  decimals.
* Tender dashboard, PDF reports, a website tenders block and a Tenders card on
  the portal home page.

Installation
------------
Requires the Python libraries openpyxl and XlsxWriter, which are installed with
Odoo.

Configuration
-------------
* In Settings > Users & Companies > Users, choose User or Administrator for
  Tender Management.
* In Settings > Tender Management, enable Auto Approval to give vendors who
  register on the website portal access at once, or choose the users who
  approve them. Auto Approval is off until you enable it.
