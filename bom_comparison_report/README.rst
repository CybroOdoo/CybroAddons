BOM Comparison Report
=====================

Compare the bills of materials of a product in a PDF report. Select two or
more bills of materials, the number of products to produce and the analysis
method, cost or sales price of the components, and get the price of the
components of each bill of materials for one product and for the products to
produce, with the most profitable one highlighted.

Key Features
------------

* Compare button on the list of the bills of materials
* Bills of materials selected in the list are prefilled in the wizard
* Analysis on the cost or on the sales price of the components
* Totals for one product and for the number of products to produce
* Quantities converted to the units of measure of the products
* Most profitable bill of materials highlighted in the PDF report
* Works with both Community and Enterprise editions

Installation
------------
No external dependencies. The module depends on Manufacturing (``mrp``).

Configuration
-------------
No additional configuration is required.
